## Design notes

**Angle.** The brief says the dog-granny videos carried the spread, and
remix_guidance names dog-owner products as a fit. The tee in
`designs/not-me-nan-tee` puts a dog in a scarf on the owner's chest. This
bandana is that scarf, made real. The owner ties it on the dog and films
their own version. It reuses the tee dog's Yolk scarf with Ink polka dots,
so the tee and the bandana sell as a matching set.

**What it shows.** A triangle bandana filled with Yolk and small Ink polka
dots. A plain Yolk panel inside the triangle holds the caption in Ink:
YOU'RE BLATANTLY / JUST NOT / ME NAN. The lines get shorter as the triangle
narrows.

**Contrast.** Ink on Yolk is about 11:1. The panel has no dots behind the
letters, so the pattern doesn't break the text.

**Print method.** Sublimation on white polyester. That's why the palette
works without an underbase. A cotton bandana would need DTG or a printer's
equivalent, so check the printer's options.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the Chalk background outside the outline. Cut to the printer's
  template, not to the drawn outline.
- Keep the caption panel centered in the visible triangle, and keep it at
  least 1 in from any seam or tie.
- One printed side only. The back can be plain Yolk or plain white.
