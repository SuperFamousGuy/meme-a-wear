## Design notes

**Product choice.** A t-shirt. The wide-armed stance needs width and the
two-figure face-off needs height, and a 12 x 16 in front fits both with the
caption underneath. A hoodie's 12 x 12 in area would shrink the figures and
crowd the caption.

**Concept.** remix_guidance says the meme lands with an original character
in the "come at me" stance facing a relatable opponent, with the caption
"What's up brah?". The opponent here is the alarm clock, the first fight of
every day. The man has just woken up: sleep mask pushed up, bathrobe,
slippers. The clock rings back at him with its own angry face. The caption
is three words and states the absurd fight flatly, in the brand voice.

**Visual signature.** The design keeps these traits from the brief:

- The forward lean with both arms flung wide and all fingers spread.
- The tilted head, the scowl with one brow angled down, and the mismatched
  round eyes.
- A thick black mustache over a small open mouth.

It changes these on purpose:

- Black hair, the teal two-pocket shirt, and the white undershirt are gone.
  They identify Randy Marsh, and the brief's `ip` field forbids him. The man
  is bald and wears a Yolk bathrobe instead.
- The flat cutout style is replaced with thick-outlined vector line art,
  because the cutout style is part of South Park's look.
- The mustache is a wide walrus shape, not the source's blocky one. A
  smaller blocky version evoked a dictator in an earlier attempt.

**Review history.** Three attempts per variant.

1. Rejected. A bald man in a yellow wrap robe with a black belt could read
   as a Buddhist monk or a martial-arts uniform.
2. Rejected. The black sleep mask read as side-swept hair, and with the
   small square mustache the face evoked a notorious dictator.
3. Accepted for light.png. A Yolk sleep mask with drawn closed eyes, a
   hooded terry bathrobe with a matching sash, and a wide walrus mustache.
4. The coordinator found that the third dark result showed a different
   face, robe, hood, and clock (outlined bells and lightning bolts). The
   designer regenerated dark.png with light.png as the only reference and a
   recolor-only prompt. It was accepted on the first try.

**Variant match.** light.png is the master. dark.png shows the same man and
the same clock in the same layout: the same face, eyes, brows, walrus
mustache, mask, hooded robe with pocket and sash, slippers, solid black
bells, key, legs, and Yolk tick sparks. The only changes are the Ink
background, a Chalk outer outline around every figure, Chalk ring lines,
and a Chalk caption. Small redraw differences remain, such as slightly
different line wobble and the ring lines merging into the clock's outline.

**Light variant.** Ink line work and an Ink caption on a flat Chalk
background. The robe, mask, slippers, and sparks are Yolk. The skin and the
clock body are Chalk. Caption contrast: Ink on white is about 18:1, on ash
about 14:1, and on sand above 10:1. Yolk never carries text.

**Dark variant.** The same art on a flat Ink background. Every figure has a
Chalk outer outline. The caption is Chalk: about 16:1 on black and above
12:1 on navy and charcoal.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the flat background from each PNG: Chalk on `light.png`, Ink on
  `dark.png`. On `light.png`, keep the Chalk fill in the skin, eyes, and
  clock face as printed Chalk on ash and sand garments.
- On `dark.png`, print the Ink shapes (mustache, bells, inner lines, clock
  legs) as Ink, not as bare fabric, so they hold on navy and charcoal.
- DTG on dark garments needs a white underbase under all printed shapes.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 12 x 16 in.
