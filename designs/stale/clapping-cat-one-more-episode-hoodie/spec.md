## Design notes

**Concept.** The brief's `remix_guidance` names "one more episode at 2 a.m."
as a caption that lands. The existing designs use a generic "bad idea" (tee)
and a coffee refill (mug). This one is the late-night couch habit, on the
garment people wear for it. The caption states the habit, then the cat's
mock-sincere approval. It names no show or service.

**Why a hoodie.** A hoodie is the product a binge-watcher wears while
binge-watching. It adds a higher-price garment to the line and a fall and
winter season fit. It doesn't duplicate the tee's angle.

**Meme traits shown.** Upright solid black cat facing the viewer, round
amber eyes, pointed ears, white-grey whiskers, a human grin with square
teeth in one flat row, and dark human hands with thumbs meeting mid-clap.
Attempt 1 drew two rows of teeth and hands held apart. Attempt 2 fixed both
by editing attempt 1. I reviewed every variant side by side with the still.

**Light variant.** Ink cat and Ink caption on a flat Chalk background.
Caption contrast: Ink on white is about 18:1, on ash or sand above 10:1.
Yolk appears only in the eyes. Smoke appears only as fur lines, whiskers, and
hand edges.

**Dark variant.** A recolor of the approved light image, so both variants
show the same cat. Ink background, cat with a Chalk outline, caption line
one in Chalk (about 18:1 on black) and line two in Yolk (about 12:1 on
black, 6:1 on charcoal). Remove the Ink background, but print the Ink fill
inside the Chalk outline as Ink, not bare fabric. DTG needs a white
underbase under all printed shapes.

**Printer notes.**

- Keep the art in the 12 x 12 in hoodie area, clear of the kangaroo pocket
  and drawstrings. The caption's bottom edge sits about 1 in above the
  area's lower edge.
- Remove the flat Chalk background before printing.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default for a hoodie front.
