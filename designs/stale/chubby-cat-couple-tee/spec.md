## Design notes

Two original round cats stand side by side and stare blankly ahead. They are one Ink cat and one
Chalk cat. The pairing carries the meme's couple format, which `remix_guidance.lands` names as the
driver of the spread, without either Outfit7 character. The cats match the mug, so the two products
sell as a set. The caption is self-directed laziness.

- **Light variant:** Ink caption on light garments exceeds 4.5:1. The Chalk cat is printed Chalk with
  Ink lines. Don't let the garment fill it. The Chalk background is removable.
- **Dark variant:** recolored from the approved light job. Chalk caption on Ink is about 16:1. The
  Ink cat has a Chalk outline. Knock out the Ink background. The printer needs a white underbase.
- The art's upper third is empty background. The printer can crop it to raise the design on the
  chest.
- The matching-couple angle sells two of one design. A one-cat-per-shirt split, such as a black-cat
  tee and a white-cat tee, is a possible follow-up if marketing wants true his-and-hers items.

**Retired 2026-10-18.** The user rejected this design after a NO-GO viability review (marketing/viability/chubby-cat-couple-tee.md): the IP-safe cats don't read as the meme, so fans won't recognize it.
