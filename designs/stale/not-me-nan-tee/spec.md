## Design notes

**Product choice.** A t-shirt. The brief calls this phrase its best apparel
fit, and the common image is a pet, not a person or character. The caption is
six words, so it reads at t-shirt scale but would get cramped on a mug's
handle-split faces or a 3 in sticker.

**What it shows.** A scruffy mixed-breed dog lies in bed with its head on a
pillow and the duvet pulled to its chest. It wears a yellow polka-dot
headscarf knotted under the chin and round black reading glasses. Both front
paws rest on the duvet. The face is flat and unbothered. A three-line caption
sits below: YOU'RE BLATANTLY / JUST NOT / ME NAN.

**Visual signature.** The design shows every trait the brief lists: a dog
dressed as an old woman with a headscarf tied under its chin, human glasses,
the dog posed like a person in bed, and the caption. One trait changes on
purpose. The meme's caption is white lowercase TikTok text. The brand sets
captions in heavy all caps, and the light variant needs Ink for contrast. The
words, including "blatantly," are unchanged, as remix_guidance requires.

**Why the caption fits.** remix_guidance names the catchphrase with an
original drawing of a dog in a headscarf and glasses as the pattern that
lands. The dark variant sets "BLATANTLY" in Yolk, the brand's one emphasis
word, because the brief says that word carries the joke.

**Light variant.** Ink line art and an Ink caption on a flat Chalk
background. Caption contrast: Ink on white is about 18:1, on ash about 14:1,
on sand above 10:1. The dog, pillow, and duvet are mostly unfilled Chalk
inside Ink lines. Yolk appears only in the scarf, never in text.

**Dark variant.** The dog, pillow, and duvet are solid Chalk shapes with Ink
line work inside and a Chalk outer edge, on a flat Ink background. "YOU'RE,"
"JUST NOT," and "ME NAN" are Chalk, about 16:1 on black and above 12:1 on
navy and charcoal. "BLATANTLY" is Yolk, about 11:1 on black, about 8:1 on
navy, and about 6:1 on charcoal.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the flat background from each PNG: Chalk on `light.png`, Ink on
  `dark.png`. On `light.png`, keep the Chalk fill inside the dog, pillow, and
  duvet as printed Chalk on ash and sand garments. Otherwise the garment
  color shows through, which is acceptable on white only.
- On `dark.png`, print the Ink lines inside the Chalk shapes as Ink, not as
  bare fabric.
- DTG on dark garments needs a white underbase under all printed shapes.
- Smoke is fur shading only. No text uses it.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 12 x 16 in.
