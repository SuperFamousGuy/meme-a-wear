## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in kiss-cut vinyl sticker shaped like a news thumbnail.

**Caption.** "Scientists reveal the" in a plain serif along the bottom, with a
rounded box holding "Hi" pasted over the last word. This is the original
meme, unchanged. The tee and mug pitches cover the garment and the "coffee"
swap; the sticker is the cheap, faithful version.

**Art direction.** The cat's face fills the frame, close up and head-on. It
keeps the brief's traits: huge round glossy black pupils that fill almost all
of each eye; a closed neutral mouth under a small nose; long whiskers that
spread past the face; the serif headline along the bottom edge; and the box
that covers the end of the headline so the last word looks cut off. Flat
vector, with a flat Ink panel behind the cat in place of the photo blur.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans aged 13 to 30. A low-cost entry and an add-on to the
tee or mug.

**Timing.** The meme placed second in KYM's October 2026 Meme of the Month
poll (2026-11-02). A sticker lists fast and needs no large run.

## Design notes

- Panel is Ink. Fur is Yolk with Ink stripes; Smoke only for whisker and fur
  detail inside Ink areas. Headline is Chalk serif on Ink, about 16:1.
- The "Hi" box is Chalk with Ink text, about 16:1. Let it run off the right
  edge of the headline, as in the still.
- Add 0.125 in bleed. Keep the headline and box at least 0.125 in inside the
  cut line.
- At 3 in, the headline is small. Keep it to the meme's three words and set
  it large enough to read at arm's length.
