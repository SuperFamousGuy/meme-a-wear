## Design notes

**Angle.** The tee in `designs/not-me-nan-tee` uses the dog-granny version
for the wearer. This mug goes back to the skit's own target: the wolf in
Nan's bed. The wolf is a public-domain folk-tale figure, and remix_pattern
lists a "wolf in a nightcap" as a valid swap. The mug suits a gift buyer,
such as a grandchild buying for a nan or a tea drinker who likes the joke.

**What it shows.** On the left, a grey wolf lies in bed with its head on a
pillow, the duvet under its chin, and both front paws over the duvet. It
wears a Yolk headscarf with Ink polka dots, knotted under the chin, and
round glasses. The scarf matches the tee dog's scarf, so the two products
read as a set. On the right is a three-line Ink caption: YOU'RE BLATANTLY /
JUST NOT / ME NAN.

**Visual signature.** The design keeps the headscarf tied under the chin,
the glasses, the animal lying in bed like a person, and the full line. It
changes one trait on purpose: the animal is a wolf, not a dog. The caption
is all caps in Ink, not white TikTok text, because white wouldn't show on a
white mug.

**Resolution.** 6336 x 2688 px across 8.5 x 3.5 in, which is about 745 x
768 DPI. No upscale needed.

**Contrast.** The caption is Ink on Chalk, about 16:1. Yolk and Smoke
appear only in non-text shapes.

**Printer notes.**

- Print the Chalk field as is. Don't remove it, since a white sublimation
  mug shows it as near-white.
- With the handle at the wrap's seam, the wolf faces a left-handed drinker
  and the caption faces a right-handed drinker. Both faces stay at least
  0.35 in from the side edges.
- Sublimation needs no underbase.
