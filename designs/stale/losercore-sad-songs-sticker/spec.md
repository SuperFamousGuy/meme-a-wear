## Design notes

**Why a sticker, and why it adds something.** The brief's audience is ages
15 to 28 and indie and alt-music listeners. The existing tee is about
being single. This sticker is about the music, the half of the meme the
tee doesn't use: the loner puts on headphones and turns up the sad songs.
A sticker suits laptops, phone cases, and guitar cases. It's also the
cheapest product, which fits a low-confidence trend.

**What it shows.** At upper left, a small hollow Chalk pictogram couple
holds hands under a tiny Yolk heart. In the foreground, a large solid Chalk
lone figure stands with his head tilted down, as in the still. He wears a
Yolk knit beanie (brief trait 4) and Chalk over-ear headphones. At the
bottom is "SAD SONGS. / FULL VOLUME." in heavy all-caps sans.

**Why it fits remix_guidance.** It's self-aware loser humor with a lone
figure next to a couple, in an original simple style. It avoids the AI
couple and any resentment toward couples or women.

**Contrast.** Chalk on Ink is about 16:1. Yolk sits only on the beanie and
heart, and Yolk on Ink is about 11:1 anyway. The caption is set in a heavy
geometric sans that matches the brand's Archivo Black rule closely enough.
Replace it with Archivo Black outlines in vector cleanup if exact type
matters.

**Resolution.** 2880 × 2880 px over 3.125 in with bleed is about 920 DPI.
No upscale needed.

**Printer notes.**

- Print the Ink background full bleed. This is a square kiss-cut sticker,
  not a die-cut.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 3 × 3 in plus 0.125 in bleed.
