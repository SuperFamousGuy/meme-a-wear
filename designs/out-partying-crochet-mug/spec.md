## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with a full-wrap print.

**Caption.** "While they're out partying btw", in two lines.

**Art direction.** The caption sits above a small icon of a niche skill, as
remix_guidance asks. It keeps the meme's look: sentence-case sans-serif in one
or two lines. Beside it stands a tall, neat stack of wrapped burgers with
crisp folded corners, the proof of hours spent. The design drops the
phone-filmed person, because the brief bans showing any creator.

**Buyer.** Fast-food workers, whom the brief lists in its audience, and the
13 to 25 viewers of the burger-wrapping clip, which passed 35 million views.
Friends buy it as a gift for the coworker who folds too neatly.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with high confidence. The burger clip
is the trend's biggest variant. A mug reaches an older buyer than the
controller hoodie.

2026-10-26: Replaced the crochet stack with wrapped burgers. The meme-expert
found no craft versions, so the viability review asked for a skill the
brief's audience already shows. Renamed the product, retargeted the buyer and
copy, and updated the brief fields.

## Design notes

- Ink caption on a removable Chalk background: about 16:1 on white ceramic.
  Yolk fills the wrappers, with Ink outlines and fold lines. Yolk never
  carries text.
- Wrappers stay blank. No chain colors, stripes, or lettering.
- No dumbbell, squat rack, or other gym props.
- Keep content 0.25 in from each edge and clear of the handle seam. Put the
  caption on the face opposite the handle and the stack beside it. Generate
  at 21:9 and pad the sides with Chalk.
