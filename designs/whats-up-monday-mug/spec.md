## Design notes

**What it adds.** The alarm-clock tee owns the "first fight of the morning"
on a garment. This design moves the same mascot to the product people hold
during that fight: the morning mug. The opponent is Monday, which
remix_guidance lists by name. The caption swaps the meme's "brah" for the
opponent's name, so the joke reads without the original audio. Sharing one
mascot across the tee, the mug, and the sticker gives marketing a product
family.

**Visual signature.** The man keeps the forward lean, both arms wide with
fingers spread, the tilted head, the scowl with one lowered brow, the
mismatched round eyes, the thick mustache, and the small open mouth. Like
the tee, it drops the black hair, the teal shirt, the undershirt, and the
cutout style on purpose, because those identify the copyrighted character.

**Layout.** On an 11 oz mug, the handle covers the seam where the left and
right edges meet. The man sits on one face and the calendar on the other.
The caption sits in the center of the wrap, on the face opposite the
handle, so the person across the table reads it. The man and the calendar
face off across the caption.

**Colors and contrast.** The caption is Ink on Chalk, about 16:1, and higher
on bare white ceramic. "MONDAY" on the calendar strip is Chalk on Ink, about
16:1. Yolk never carries text.

**Resolution.** 3840 x 1648 px over 8.5 x 3.5 in is about 452 x 471 DPI. No
upscale needed.

**Printer notes.**

- Sublimation can't print white. The printer can remove the Chalk
  background and let the white mug show. Chalk fills inside the figures
  then print as bare white, which reads the same.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 8.5 x 3.5 in for an 11 oz wrap.
