## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 x 3 in die-cut sticker.

**Caption.** "INSTEAD OF ZOMBIES IT'S A GROUP PROJECT", seven words, in a
light caption box.

**Art direction.** The design keeps the brief's traits: a plain sans-serif
text block on a light caption box that swaps a classic scare for an everyday
one. Above the box sits a small original haunted house with a steep roof and
Yolk-lit windows. In one window, four tiny stick-figure silhouettes sit at a
desk, and only one has a raised hand. The house replaces the template's
opening line on purpose, to fit the size.

**Buyer.** Students aged 16 to 24, the brief's younger audience. They put it
on laptops and water bottles. It's cheap enough to buy on a whim.

**Timing.** KYM dated the mainstream wave to this month. A sticker has the
lowest cost and the shortest production of any product, so it has the best
chance of selling before 2026-10-31. If it misses, it fits October 2027.

## Design notes

- Background: Chalk with a die-cut border. Ink house and caption text on the
  Chalk box, about 16:1. Yolk windows only. Yolk never carries text.
- Add 0.125 in bleed. Generate at 1:1.
- The window figures must be simple silhouettes, not people anyone could
  recognize.
