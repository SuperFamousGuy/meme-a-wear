## Pitch

**Product.** An insulated zip lunch bag with one print on the front panel.

**Caption.** "W chews". The brief's remix_guidance names "W chews" on a
lunch bag as a pattern that lands. One phrase, no explanation.

**Art direction.** The brief's traits keep the phrase in plain bold sans, a
capital W with the rest lowercase, above a meal. Under the phrase, draw the
heaped rice bowl from the brief's still as a flat icon: glossy glaze, sesame
dots, and scallion rings. It's the same bowl as the W chews sticker and
magnet, so the products read as one line. Changes: a flat icon replaces the
food photo.

**Buyer.** Students aged 13 to 25 who bring lunch and already use W and L
slang. Parents buying back-to-school gear for teens who say it.

**Timing.** The brief moved to peaking on 2026-11-16, with explainers on
KYM, X, and YouTube. A lunch bag is used daily, so it keeps the phrase in
view while it is current.

## Design notes

- Ink panel, Chalk text, Yolk glaze and sesame dots. Chalk on Ink is about
  16:1. Sublimation needs a polyester panel. Fill the whole panel with Ink
  rather than relying on the bag color.
- Keep the phrase and bowl clear of the zipper, seams, and handle by at
  least 0.5 in, or the printer's own safe zone if larger.
- Generate at the panel's aspect ratio once the template is known.
