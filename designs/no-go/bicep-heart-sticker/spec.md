## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in kiss-cut vinyl sticker, cut to the icon's contour.

**Caption.** None. The icon carries the gesture. The brief's remix_guidance
names a simple drawn icon of the arm and heart as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** A tight gesture icon. It keeps every brief trait: one arm
bent sharply at the elbow, forearm raised, biceps bulging toward the viewer;
that wrist bends forward so the curled fingers point back down toward the
biceps and form one top lobe of a heart; the free hand curves into a "C",
its fingertips meeting the flexed hand's at the top, its thumb and palm on
the biceps; the gap they frame is a heart pointing at the biceps. The heart
fills with Yolk. It changes the live-action arm to a neutral Ink line icon
with no skin tone.

**Buyer.** Gym-goers aged 16 to 30, for water bottles, gym bags, and
lockers. A cheap test of a young trend.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with medium confidence, verified
2026-11-09T23:12Z. A sticker lists fastest while the trend is new.

**Revised 2026-11-09.** Matched the art direction and source_brief to the
re-verified brief. Switched to kiss-cut, which keeps the margin positive.

## Design notes

- Ink line art on a Chalk face, Yolk heart fill. Ink on Chalk is about 16:1.
- Kiss-cut along the icon's contour with a Chalk border at least 0.125 in
  wide. The backing stays square. Add 0.125 in bleed past the cut line.
- Keep line weights at least 0.04 in at print size.
- The heart gap must read at 3 in. Check it before anything else; without
  the heart, the brief says the pose reads as a plain flex.
- One variant: `sticker.png`. The sibling tee is no-go, so this icon has no
  other product to match.
