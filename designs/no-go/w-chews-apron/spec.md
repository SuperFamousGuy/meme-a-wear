## Pitch

**Product.** A bib apron with a front chest print.

**Caption.** "W chews" only. The brief's remix_guidance names "W chews" on an
apron as a pattern that lands. A cook wears the win, never the loss, so this
product drops "L nibbles".

**Art direction.** The phrase large, with the capital W and lowercase
"chews" the brief's traits describe. Above it sits the heaped rice bowl icon
from the mug and sticker pitches: glossy glazed topping, sesame dots, and
scallion rings, redrawn from the meal in the brief's still as a flat icon.
Same bowl across the line, so the products read as a set.

**Buyer.** Ages 16 to 25 who cook for friends and post their food, and
people buying a gift for the household cook. It adds a kitchen product to the
mug and sticker set.

**Timing.** The brief moved to peaking on 2026-11-06, after a KYM roundup and
explainer. Gift season is next, and aprons are a common gift.

## Design notes

- Light apron (natural, white): Ink text and bowl outline, Yolk glaze.
  Yolk is a non-text shape here.
- Dark apron (black): Chalk text and bowl outline, Yolk glaze. Give any Ink
  shape that touches the fabric a Chalk outline of at least 0.12 in.
- Keep the print clear of the strap seams and any front pocket.
- Variants: `light.png` and `dark.png`, as for garments.
