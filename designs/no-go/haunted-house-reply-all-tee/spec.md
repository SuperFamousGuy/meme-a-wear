## Pitch

**Product.** A front-print t-shirt in light and dark variants.

**Caption.** "INSTEAD OF ZOMBIES IT'S REPLY-ALL", seven words, in a light
caption box.

**Art direction.** The design keeps the brief's traits: a plain sans-serif
text block on a light caption box that swaps a classic scare for an everyday
one. Behind the box sits an original two-story haunted house with a steep
roof, bats, and Yolk-lit windows. One window glows with a tiny envelope
icon. The house replaces the opening line of the template, which changes the
first trait on purpose to fit the brand's caption limit.

**Buyer.** Office workers aged 22 to 35 who live in shared inboxes. It's a
Halloween-office-party shirt and a coworker gift. The joke is a shared
annoyance, so nobody feels targeted.

**Timing.** KYM dated the mainstream wave to this month (2026-10-20 and
2026-10-23). The window closes on 2026-10-31. If it can't ship in time, it
fits October 2027.

## Design notes

- **Light variant:** Ink house and bats, Yolk windows, Chalk caption box with
  an Ink outline and Ink text. **Dark variant:** Chalk outlines on the house
  at least 0.12 in thick, Yolk windows, Chalk box with Ink text.
- Ink on Chalk is about 16:1. Yolk never carries text.
- Generate at 3:4. Avoid fog or moonlight gradients near edges.
