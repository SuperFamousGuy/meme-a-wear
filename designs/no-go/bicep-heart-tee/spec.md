## Pitch

**Product.** A crew-neck t-shirt with a front-center print.

**Caption.** "SEND BICEP HEARTS" below the icon. The brief's remix_guidance
names this short text with the icon as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** A bold, flat icon of the gesture, sized like a chest
badge. It keeps every brief trait: one arm bent at the elbow in a flex,
biceps toward the viewer; that hand's fingers point back down toward its own
forearm, leaving a gap; the other hand forms a "C" that closes the gap; arm
and hands together outline a heart. The heart's inside fills with Yolk, so
the shape reads before the arm does. It changes the live-action arm to a
neutral, stylized line icon with no skin tone, torso, or face.

**Buyer.** Gym-goers and couples aged 16 to 30 on TikTok and Reels. It's a
wholesome flex shirt that says the trend without a selfie.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking: tens of millions of views and an
explainer within a week of 2026-10-31. Confidence is low, so price it as a
quick test.

## Design notes

- The arm and hands are a thick line drawing in Ink on light garments and
  Chalk on dark garments, with no skin fill. That keeps it neutral for every
  buyer.
- The heart fill is Yolk. On light garments, Yolk is a non-text shape only.
- Caption in Archivo Black, all caps: Ink on light (about 16:1), Chalk on
  dark (about 16:1).
- Keep line weights at least 0.08 in at print size so the hands survive DTG.
- Test the icon at 2 in wide. If the heart doesn't read, simplify the
  fingers before adding detail.
