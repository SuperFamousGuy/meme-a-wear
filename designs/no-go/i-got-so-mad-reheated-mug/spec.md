## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with two print panels, one on each side of the
handle.

**Caption.** Side A says "I GOT SO MAD I REHEATED" on three short lines:
"I GOT SO", "MAD I", "REHEATED". The mug in the drinker's hand is what got
reheated. The brief's remix_guidance names text-led mugs with a trivial act
as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** The brief's traits put the caption above the small,
ordinary object the act hit, shown flat on a plain surface. Side B shows that
object: a small generic microwave on a flat counter line, its window lit
Yolk, with a tiny mug inside. The mug inside is the same shape as the real
one, so the joke loops back to the buyer's own cup. Changes: a flat vector
icon replaces the phone photo, and the caption drops its drop shadow.

**Buyer.** Students and young workers aged 18 to 25 who reheat the same
coffee all morning. A desk-mate gift.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging and close to peaking. A text-led mug
lists fast, before the peak.

**Revised 2026-11-16.** Updated the IP notes and open risks to the brief's
re-check: no US mark found on a mirror, EU unchecked. Cut "IT" so the
caption is six words, which meets the brand voice.

## Design notes

- The wrap prints on white ceramic. The Chalk background is removable.
- Caption and microwave outline are Ink, about 16:1 on white. Yolk fills only
  the microwave window glow and one steam curl, never text.
- Center each panel on its side, facing out when held in either hand. Keep
  content 0.25 in from each edge and clear of the handle seam.
- No digits or text on the microwave display. Keep it a blank Ink rectangle.
- Generate each panel at about 1:1 and place them on one 8.5 × 3.5 in wrap.
