## Pitch

**Product.** Two 3 x 3 in die-cut fridge magnets, sold as a set.

**Caption.** Magnet one says "W chews". Magnet two says "L nibbles". The
brief's remix_guidance names a two-sided rating as a pattern that lands. A
fridge is where food gets rated, and a magnet lets the verdict move to a new
container each day.

**Art direction.** The brief's traits keep the phrase in plain bold sans, a
capital W or L with the rest lowercase, above a meal. Each magnet is cut to
the shape of a bowl, taken from the glazed rice bowl in the brief's still and
redrawn as a flat icon. "W chews" is a heaped bowl with a glossy glaze and
sesame dots. "L nibbles" is the same bowl, nearly empty, with one sad piece
left. The phrase sits on the bowl's front.

**Buyer.** Roommates, shared-house students, and families with teens aged
13 to 25 who already use W and L slang. A cheap gift.

**Timing.** The brief moved to peaking on 2026-11-16: explainers on KYM, X,
and YouTube, across four platforms. Magnets reach buyers now.

## Design notes

- "W chews" magnet: Ink bowl body, Chalk text, Yolk glaze and sesame dots.
  Chalk on Ink is about 16:1.
- "L nibbles" magnet: Chalk bowl body with an Ink outline, Ink text. No
  Yolk text on Chalk.
- The die-cut line follows each bowl's outline plus a 0.1 in Chalk border.
  Add 0.125 in bleed past the cut line. Keep the text at least 0.125 in
  inside the cut line.
- Both magnets use the same bowl outline, size, and text position, so they
  read as a pair.
- Variants: one file per magnet face, `w.png` and `l.png`.
