## Design notes

**Why a mug, and why it adds something.** The existing tee
(`designs/losercore-table-for-one-tee`) labels the wearer "LOSERCORE". This
mug drops the label and gives the loner a payoff. He isn't missing out, he
has a date: the mug you're holding. The joke lands on the physical object,
which a tee can't do. It's also a gift item, for "the single friend" at
holidays, so it reaches a buyer the tee doesn't.

**What it shows.** On the left, a hollow Ink pictogram couple holds hands
under a Yolk heart. In the center is "MY DATE / IS THIS MUG" in heavy
all-caps sans. On the right, a solid Ink lone figure with his head tilted
down holds a small mug with Yolk steam. His lowered head comes from the
still.

**Why it fits remix_guidance.** It uses the lone figure beside a couple in
an original simple style. It avoids the pink-and-blue couple and any
resentment toward couples or women. The loner is a little sad and fine with
it.

**Contrast.** The caption is Ink on Chalk, about 16:1. Yolk appears only as
the heart and the steam, never as text. The caption is set in a heavy
geometric sans that matches the brand's Archivo Black rule closely enough.
Replace it with Archivo Black outlines in vector cleanup if exact type
matters.

**Resolution.** 3840 × 1648 px over 8.5 × 3.5 in is about 450 DPI wide and
470 DPI tall, above the 300 DPI target. No upscale needed.

**Printer notes.**

- Keep the figures and caption where they are. Each outer figure sits about
  0.87 in from its edge, clear of the 0.25 in limit and the handle.
- Extend the background to the full 8.5 in width rather than stretching
  (see open_risks).
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 8.5 × 3.5 in.
