## Design notes

**Why this adds something.** tra-la-la-blob-tee covers the core angle: the
blob as the wearer's unhinged mood, in a tall print. This mug changes two
things. It sells a different product type, at a gift price point and to
office and dorm buyers who don't wear meme tees. It also adds a new joke:
the unhinged singing happens at 7 AM, where a mug lives. The 8.5 × 3.5 in
wrap also fits the brief's first trait, a sideways stretch, better than the
tee's tall diagonal.

**What it shows.** On the left, an original yellow blob stretches
horizontally. Its huge mouth opens to the left, with an Ink interior, a
Chalk tongue, and separated square Chalk teeth (two above, three below). Two
round googly eyes sit squeezed together at the top tip. The body melts into
a wavy tail that runs right, toward the caption. Motion lines show that it
sings. On the right, the caption reads "TRA LA LA." in large type and "IT IS
7 AM." below it, both in Ink.

**Visual signature.** The design keeps traits one to three: the long melted
sideways body, the huge open mouth with a tongue, and the big split square
teeth. It keeps trait four in part: two round eyes squeezed at the top. It
changes these on purpose, for the same reasons as the tee:

- The character is an original blob, not SpongeBob, as the `ip` field
  requires.
- The eyes have Ink pupils with no blue irises and no lashes.
- The tongue is Chalk, not pink, to stay in the brand palette.
- Trait five, the pineapple house and sky, is dropped. That background is
  protected art.

**Why the caption fits.** remix_guidance names "tra la la" as the text hook.
"IT IS 7 AM." states the absurd situation as plain fact, which is the brand's
deadpan voice. The caption is six words, inside the brand's eight-word limit.

**Contrast.** The caption is Ink on Chalk, about 16:1, or about 18:1 on a
bare white mug. Yolk appears only as a body fill, never as text.

**Printer notes.**

- Sublimation can't print white. Either print the Chalk background as a
  full-wrap tint, or drop it to bare white mug. If you drop it, the teeth,
  eyes, and tongue print as bare white, which is acceptable.
- The handle sits at the wrap seam, at the left and right edges of the art.
  The nearest content is the caption, about 0.28 in from the right edge.
- Scale the art to 8.5 in wide and center it vertically. This trims about
  0.07 in of empty margin from the top and bottom.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 8.5 × 3.5 in.

**Concepts considered and not made.** A sticker would cut the tee's art down
to 3 × 3 in and add no new angle. The tee spec already lists it as a
follow-up product, so it doesn't need a new concept. A hoodie would repeat
the tee.
