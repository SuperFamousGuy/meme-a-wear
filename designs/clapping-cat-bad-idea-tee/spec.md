## Design notes

**What it shows.** A slim, black short-hair cat sits upright and faces the
viewer, above a two-line caption. It has angular ears, a narrow muzzle, and a
visible neck. Its yellow-amber eyes are half closed under heavy lids, which
gives a smug look. A small human mouth on the muzzle shows one flat row of
square Chalk teeth in a tight grin. Two human hands, fur-black, clap in front
of the chest with fingers spread.

**Why it reads as the meme.** The uncanny effect comes from human teeth and
human hands on a realistic cat. The prompts ask for those two traits to carry
the most contrast.

**Why the caption fits.** The brief's `remix_guidance` says captions about
being cheered on for something small or bad land. "BAD IDEA. LOVE IT." is the
cat's applause put into words. It works without the video. The caption is
original.

**Light variant.** An Ink cat and an Ink caption on a flat Chalk background.
Caption contrast: Ink on white is about 18:1, and Ink on sand stays above
10:1.

**Dark variant.** An Ink cat with a thick Chalk outline on a flat Ink
background, so the cat stays visible on black. "BAD IDEA." is Chalk and
"LOVE IT." is Yolk. Caption contrast: Chalk on black is about 18:1. Yolk on
black is about 12:1, on navy about 8:1, and on charcoal about 6:1.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the flat background from each PNG (Chalk on `light.png`, Ink on
  `dark.png`) before printing. On `dark.png`, keep the Ink fill inside the
  cat's Chalk outline. Print it as Ink, not as bare fabric.
- DTG on dark garments needs a white underbase under all printed shapes.
- Smoke is shading only. No text uses it.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 12 × 16 in.
