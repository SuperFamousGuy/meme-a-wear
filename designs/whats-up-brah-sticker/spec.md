## Design notes

**What it adds.** The alarm-clock tee and the Monday mug both pick the
opponent. In the meme, the joke is that Randy squares up to anything. This
sticker shows the man alone, so the buyer supplies the opponent by where
they stick it: facing a laptop camera, a fridge, a monitor, a water bottle.
The meme's swap-the-opponent remix becomes something the buyer does. It's
also the lowest-cost entry product for the brief's 13 to 35 audience.

**Visual signature.** The figure keeps the forward lean, both arms wide with
fingers spread, the tilted head, the scowl with one lowered brow, the
mismatched round eyes, the thick mustache, and the small open mouth. Like
the tee, it drops the black hair, the teal shirt, the undershirt, and the
cutout style on purpose. The opponent from remix_pattern is left out on
purpose: the buyer's surface replaces it.

**Colors and contrast.** The caption is Ink on the Chalk border, about 16:1.
Yolk carries no text. Smoke appears only as one small line under the mouth.

**Resolution.** 2880 x 2880 px over 3 x 3 in is 960 DPI. No upscale needed.

**Printer notes.**

- Cut along the outer edge of the Chalk border. Discard the Ink area
  outside it. Add 0.125 in bleed past the cut line.
- The Chalk border prints as white vinyl or as Chalk ink. Either reads the
  same.
- No printer template was used. The size follows the `design-spec` default
  of 3 x 3 in.
