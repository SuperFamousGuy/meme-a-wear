## Design notes

The wrap places the imp on the left third, sneaking toward a two-line caption
on the right. The handle sits at the seam where the two short edges meet, so
the imp shows on one side of the mug and the caption on the other. The empty
center strip faces away from the handle. Both elements keep a wide margin from
the seam. The
caption follows the brief's remix_pattern: "tip toein'" plus a sneaky
situation, here avoiding email.

Resolution: 3840 px / 8.5 in is about 452 DPI. 1648 px / 3.5 in is about 471
DPI. No upscale needed.

Printing: the Chalk (#F2F2EE) background prints as near-white on a white
sublimation mug. The printer may drop it to white. Key content sits well over
0.25 in from each edge. The caption was checked letter by letter: T-I-P
T-O-E-I-N-' / P-A-S-T M-Y I-N-B-O-X.
