## Pitch

**Product.** A front-print t-shirt with a light and a dark variant.

**Caption.** "I GOT SO MAD I MUTED", set on two lines: "I GOT SO MAD" and
"I MUTED". The headset below shows what got muted, as the meme's photo shows
the object. The brief's remix_guidance names text-led gamer shirts with a
trivial act as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** The brief's traits keep the caption above a small,
ordinary object that the act hit, shown flat and close up on a plain
surface. Below the caption, draw a generic gaming headset lying flat on a
short tabletop line, its mic boom flipped up, with a small Yolk mute badge (a
circle with a slash) beside it. Changes: a flat vector icon replaces the
phone photo, and the caption drops its drop shadow, which only helped
legibility over photos.

**Buyer.** Gamers aged 13 to 25 who mute mid-match instead of yelling.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging and close to peaking, with a KYM
explainer on 2026-11-16. A tee can list before the peak.

**Revised 2026-11-16.** Updated the IP notes and open risks to the brief's
re-check: no US mark found on a mirror, EU unchecked. Cut the caption from
seven words to six to meet the brand voice. The mute badge now carries "my
mic".

## Design notes

- Caption is about 10 in wide, with the headset icon about 5 in wide below
  it. Total height about 9 in, centered in the 12 × 16 in area.
- Light variant: Ink caption and headset outline on white, ash, or sand.
  Yolk only for the mute badge fill, never text.
- Dark variant: Chalk caption and headset outline on black, navy, or
  charcoal. Ink fills inside the headset get a Chalk outline at least 0.12 in
  thick.
- The tabletop line is one flat Ink (light) or Chalk (dark) stroke. No wood
  grain, gradient, or soft shadow.
- Generate at 3:4 to match the print area.
