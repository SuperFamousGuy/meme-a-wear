## Pitch

**Product.** A crew-neck t-shirt with a front-center print, light and dark
variants.

**Caption.** I GOT SO MAD I SAID "NO WORRIES" (seven words). The brief's
remix_guidance names short petty lines as text slogans, and a drawing of a
snapped object under the caption.

**Art direction.** Keep: a caption that starts "I got so mad I", centered
above the aftermath, and a broken everyday object seen from above on a flat
surface. The object is a yellow pencil snapped in two, with a few
splinters. Change: all-caps Archivo Black, per the brand. Flat vector, no
photo.

**Buyer.** Students and young workers aged 18 to 30 who answer every
annoyance with a polite reply. The joke is a familiar act of suppressed
rage.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking, with KYM coverage through
2026-11-20. The "no worries" joke outlives the trend, so stock stays
sellable if the meme fades.

## Design notes

- Light garments: Ink caption, about 16:1. Pencil body in Yolk with Ink
  outlines and an Ink graphite tip.
- Dark garments: Chalk caption with a thin Ink outline, matching the meme.
  Pencil outlines in Chalk at least 0.12 in thick. DTG needs a white
  underbase.
- Never set caption text in Yolk on light garments.
- Generate at 3:4.
