## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in die-cut vinyl sticker.

**Caption.** "*PAWS AT YOU*" arched over the leopard's head, with the
asterisks. The brief's remix_guidance names the caption with its asterisks as
a pattern that lands. No added request, so it suits any buyer, gamer or not.

**Art direction.** A head-and-paw crop of a plush-cute snow leopard facing
straight on. It keeps the brief's traits: one front paw pushed toward the
viewer, drawn oversized with four round toe pads and a big main pad; huge
round glossy dark eyes; a small nose and closed neutral mouth low on a short
face; thin whiskers; small spots on the head and larger blotches on the
raised leg. The paw overlaps the bottom of the face, as in the still. Flat
vector, no background.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans and gamers aged 15 to 30. A sticker is the cheapest
way to own the meme while it peaks, and a natural add-on to the hoodie.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking. A sticker can list fast, before the
trend saturates.

## Design notes

- Fur is Chalk with Smoke shading. Spots, eyes, nose, paw pads, and caption
  are Ink. Yolk appears only as a small catchlight in each eye.
- Give the leopard a thin Ink outline so the Chalk fur reads against the
  Chalk die-cut border.
- Add 0.125 in bleed. Keep the caption and paw at least 0.125 in inside the
  cut line.
- The caption must stay legible at 3 in. Keep it to the two-word phrase plus
  asterisks.
