## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in die-cut vinyl sticker.

**Caption.** "*PAWS AT YOU*" arched over the leopard's head, with the
asterisks. The brief's remix_guidance names the caption with its asterisks as
a pattern that lands. No added request, so it suits any buyer, gamer or not.

**Art direction.** A head-and-paw crop of a plush-cute snow leopard facing
straight on. It keeps the brief's traits: one front paw pushed toward the
viewer, drawn oversized with four round toe pads and a big main pad; huge
round glossy dark eyes; a small nose and closed neutral mouth low on a short
face; thin whiskers; small spots on the head and larger blotches on the
raised leg. The paw overlaps the bottom of the face, as in the still. Flat
vector, no background.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans and gamers aged 15 to 30. A sticker is the cheapest
way to own the meme while it peaks, and a natural add-on to the hoodie.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking. A sticker can list fast, before the
trend saturates.

## Design notes

- Fur is Chalk with Smoke shading. Spots, eyes, nose, paw pads, and caption
  are Ink. Yolk appears only as a small catchlight in each eye.
- Give the leopard a thin Ink outline so the Chalk fur reads against the
  Chalk die-cut border.
- Add 0.125 in bleed. Keep the caption and paw at least 0.125 in inside the
  cut line.
- The caption must stay legible at 3 in. Keep it to the two-word phrase plus
  asterisks.

### Artwork (sticker.svg)

- **Source.** Hand-written SVG fallback. Higgsfield had no image credits on
  2026-11-02. See `open_risks`.
- **Traits kept.** A pale leopard faces straight on with rounded ears. One
  oversized front paw pushes at the viewer, with four round Ink toe pads and a
  large Ink main pad. The eyes are huge, round, glossy Ink circles with a
  Smoke rim, one Yolk catchlight, and a small Chalk glint. A small Ink nose
  and a closed neutral mouth sit low on a short face, with thin Smoke
  whiskers. Small Ink spots cover the head. Larger Ink blotches cover the
  chest and the raised leg.
- **Changes from the still, per the pitch.** Head-and-paw crop. The caption
  is "*PAWS AT YOU*" in Archivo Black caps, arched over the head, with the
  asterisks. The nose is Ink instead of grey. The paw overlaps the chin by
  about 0.07 in. In the still, the paw sits at chest level below the chin.
- **Layout.** The canvas is 3.25 × 3.25 in: a 3 in trim plus 0.125 in bleed
  on each side. The `CutContour` layer is the die-cut line. It follows the
  caption arc on top and the head and body below. It sits at least 0.14 in
  outside the art, and the Chalk bleed extends 0.125 in past it.
  `CutContour` is a non-printing magenta hairline. Hide it before printing,
  or map it to the printer's cut spot color.
- **Resolution.** Vector, so it scales without loss. The thinnest lines are
  the whiskers, at 0.012 in.
- **Contrast.** The Ink caption on the Chalk border is about 16:1. Yolk
  appears only as the eye catchlights, inside Ink. No text uses Smoke or
  Yolk.
- **Spelling.** The caption source text is `*PAWS AT YOU*`.
