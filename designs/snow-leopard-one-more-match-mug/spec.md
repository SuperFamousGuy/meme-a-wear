## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug. One panel prints twice, once on each side of the
handle, so any single view shows the full joke.

**Caption.** "*PAWS AT YOU*" over "ONE MORE MATCH", beside the leopard.
The brief's remix_guidance names this pattern, and says the caption without
the paw falls flat. The asterisks stay.

**Art direction.** The panel's left half shows the plush-cute snow leopard,
head and raised paw, facing straight on. It keeps the brief's traits: one
front paw pushed toward the viewer, oversized, with four round toe pads and a
big main pad; huge round glossy dark eyes; a small grey nose and closed
neutral mouth low on a short face; thin whiskers; small spots on the head and
larger blotches on the raised leg. It changes the photo to flat art with no
background.

**Buyer.** Gamers aged 15 to 30 who play late. The desk mug asks for one
more match on their behalf, next to the hoodie and sticker.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking, verified 2026-11-09T23:12Z. Volume is
likely cooling, so a mug is a lower-cost test than more apparel.

**Revised 2026-11-09.** Put the caption on the leopard's panel and repeated
the panel on both sides. The marketing review found that one view showed
half the joke.

## Design notes

- White mug. Fur is Chalk with Smoke shading and an Ink outline, so it reads
  on the white ceramic. Spots, eyes, nose, and pads are Ink. Yolk appears
  only as a small catchlight in each eye.
- Caption in Ink, Archivo Black, all caps, about 16:1 on the white mug. Set
  it in two lines on the panel's right half, beside the paw.
- Each panel is about 3.75 × 3 in: the wrap split in two, minus the handle
  gap and the 0.25 in edge margins. Make the paw the largest element. It
  must stay big on a panel this size.
- Keep key content 0.25 in from each edge and clear of the handle.
- Reuse the hoodie pitch's leopard drawing so the line matches. One variant:
  `wrap.png`, with the same panel placed twice.
