## Design notes

**Concept.** The brief's core joke is sarcastic applause for a bad decision.
A sticker carries that applause anywhere: on a laptop during a deadline, on
the fridge, on a water bottle. "GREAT CHOICE." fits any surface, so the
buyer supplies the bad decision. The existing tee and mug each fix one
context; this one leaves it open.

**Why a sticker.** The brief's confidence is medium, with no source newer
than 2026-10-08. A sticker is the lowest-cost, fastest product in the line.
It lets marketing test demand for the meme before more garment stock. It
also suits the brief's 13-30 audience as an impulse add-on.

**Meme traits shown.** Upright solid black cat facing the viewer, round
amber eyes, pointed ears, whiskers, a human grin with square teeth, and two
dark human hands overlapping mid-clap. The hands and grin are drawn large,
as `remix_guidance` asks. Attempt 1 drew two rows of teeth. Attempt 2 fixed
the mouth by editing attempt 1 and left everything else unchanged. I
reviewed it side by side with the still.

**Colors and contrast.** Caption is Yolk on an Ink banner, about 11:1. No
text sits on a light surface. Smoke is shading only.

**Printer notes.**

- Print on white vinyl. The Chalk die-cut border prints as a light tint, or
  the printer can leave it as bare white vinyl.
- Resolution: 2880 px over 3 in is 960 DPI, far above 300 DPI. The sticker
  shape fills about three quarters of the square image, so scale it to the
  3 in size after trimming to the border.
- No printer template was used. The size follows the `design-spec` default
  for a sticker.
