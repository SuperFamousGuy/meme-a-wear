## Design notes

Two original round cats stand side by side and stare blankly ahead. They are one Ink cat and one
Chalk cat. This keeps the meme's couple pairing, which `remix_guidance.lands` names as the driver of
the spread, without either Outfit7 character. The caption is self-directed laziness.

- Effective resolution is about 452 DPI wide and 471 DPI tall, so no upscale is needed.
- The caption sits at the left, and the cats sit center-right. The left margin is about 0.5 in, and
  the right margin is wider. Both clear the 0.25 in edge rule. The handle sits at the seam, so a
  right-handed drinker sees the cats, and a left-handed drinker sees the caption.
- Ink caption on a white mug is about 16:1. Yolk appears only in the eyes, never as text.
- The Chalk background can print as is on a white mug or drop out to the mug's white.
