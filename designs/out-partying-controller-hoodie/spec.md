## Pitch

**Product.** A front-print hoodie in light and dark variants.

**Caption.** "While they're out partying btw", in two lines.

**Art direction.** The brief's remix_guidance asks for the caption as a
slogan above a small icon of a niche skill, and names a game controller. The
design keeps the caption's look: white sans-serif in sentence case with a
thin dark outline, in one or two lines. Under it sits a generic controller
with its thumbsticks worn smooth and a small Yolk spark of pride. The design
drops the third trait, the phone-filmed person, on purpose. The brief bans
showing any creator, and the controller stands in for the skill.

**Buyer.** Gamers aged 13 to 25, the brief's main crossover audience. They
buy it to brag about a useless skill with a straight face. A hoodie suits
long sessions indoors.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with medium confidence. Viral clips
passed 35 million views from 2026-10-18, and KYM explained the meme on
2026-10-23. Hoodie season starts now.

## Design notes

- **Light variant:** Ink caption, Ink controller with Smoke wear marks, Yolk
  spark. **Dark variant:** Chalk caption with a thin Ink outline, matching
  the meme, and the controller with a Chalk outline at least 0.12 in thick.
- Contrast: Ink on light garments and Chalk on dark garments are about 16:1.
- Keep the art inside the 12 x 12 in area and clear of the pocket and
  drawstrings. Generate at 1:1.
