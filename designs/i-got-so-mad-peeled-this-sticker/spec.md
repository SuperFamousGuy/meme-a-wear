## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 x 3 in square kiss-cut sticker with rounded corners.

**Caption.** "I GOT SO MAD I PEELED" on three lines: "I GOT SO", "MAD I",
"PEELED". The curled corner shows what got peeled. The brief's
remix_guidance names a trivial act as the core pattern.

**Art direction.** The brief's traits put the caption over a small, ordinary
object the act hit, shown flat on a plain surface. Here the object is the
sticker itself: the art draws its bottom-right corner curled up, showing an
Ink underside, as if someone peeled it in a rage and stopped. The buyer's
laptop or notebook becomes the plain surface from the meme's photo. Changes:
the drawn sticker replaces the phone photo, and the caption drops its drop
shadow.

**Buyer.** Teens and students aged 13 to 25 who know the trend and fill
laptops and water bottles with stickers. A cheap add-on to the tee or mug.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging and close to peaking. A sticker is
the fastest product to list while the format is new.

**Revised 2026-11-16.** Updated the IP notes and open risks to the brief's
re-check: no US mark found on a mirror, EU unchecked. Cut "THIS" so the
caption is six words, which meets the brand voice.
