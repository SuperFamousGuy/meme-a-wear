## Pitch

**Product.** A die-cut fridge magnet, 3 × 1.5 in, cut to the notification box.

**Caption.** Title: "DO THE DISHES". Tag line: "+♥ Mom will appreciate this".
Instruction: "Yes, the pan too." The brief's remix_guidance names chore jokes
and a name swap on the tag line as patterns that land.

**Art direction.** Keep the brief's traits: a dark rectangular box, a thin
colored line along the top edge, a bold condensed all-caps title, a small
"+" and solid heart before a rounded sans tag line, and one sentence-case
instruction line. Change: Ink box instead of navy, Yolk instead of pink, and
no portrait square. Flat vector, no photo texture.

**Buyer.** Gamers aged 16 to 35 and their families. A cheap add-on gift that
lives where the chore happens.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with high confidence, launched with
the game on 2026-11-19. A low-cost magnet can list fast and sell through
before the meme fades.

## Design notes

- Die-cut along the box edge with 0.125 in bleed. Ink fills the box.
- Title and lines are Chalk on Ink, about 16:1. Yolk fills the top line, the
  "+", and the heart. Yolk on Ink is about 11:1.
- At 3 in wide, the instruction line must stay at least 7 pt. Keep it to one
  line.
- Generate at about 2:1 and request a flat card on a solid Chalk background
  that the printer can remove.
