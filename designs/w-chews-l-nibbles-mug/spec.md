## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with two print panels, one on each side of the
handle.

**Caption.** Side A says "W chews". Side B says "L nibbles". The brief's
remix_guidance names "W chews" on a mug and a two-sided rating card as
patterns that land. This mug is both.

**Art direction.** Side A shows a simple, heaped rice bowl icon: a glossy
glazed topping, sesame dots, and scallion rings, taken from the meal in the
brief's still. Side B shows the same bowl nearly empty, with one small sad
piece left. Each phrase sits above its bowl in plain bold sans, as the brief's
traits describe: a capital W or L, the rest lowercase. Flat vector, no photo.

**Buyer.** Ages 13 to 25 who already rate things W or L, and food posters. A
gift for the friend who reviews every meal out loud.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging on three platforms, with KYM coverage
starting 2026-11-02. A text-led mug can list before the trend peaks.

## Design notes

- The wrap prints on white ceramic. The Chalk background is removable.
- Text and bowl outlines are Ink, about 16:1 on white. Yolk fills only the
  glaze on side A, never text.
- Center each panel on its side, facing away from the handle when held in
  either hand. Keep content 0.25 in from each edge and clear of the handle
  seam.
- Generate each panel separately at about 1:1 and place them on one
  8.5 × 3.5 in wrap, or generate one 21:9 wrap with both panels.

### Artwork (wrap.svg)

- **Source.** Hand-written SVG fallback, so the file is `wrap.svg`, not
  PNG. Higgsfield had no image credits on 2026-11-02. See `open_risks`.
- **Traits kept.** The phrases "W chews" and "L nibbles", with a capital W
  or L and the rest lowercase, in plain bold sans above a meal. The meal is
  the still's bowl: a white bowl heaped with glazed, sesame-topped chunks
  over rice.
- **Changes from the still, per the pitch.** The "W chews or L nibbles"
  choice splits into two panels. The meal is a flat icon. The glaze is
  Yolk, the sesame is Ink, and the scallions are left out to keep the
  three-color palette, as on the sticker set.
- **Match with the sticker set.** Both panels reuse the bowl, chunk, and
  caption geometry of `designs/w-chews-l-nibbles-button-set/w.svg` and
  `l.svg`, scaled 1.3x. Side B is the `l.svg` bowl unchanged: an Ink bowl,
  empty except for one tilted Yolk chunk and three seeds. Side A is the
  `w.svg` heaped bowl, with an Ink outline added to the bowl, rim, and foot
  so the Chalk bowl reads on a white mug. Both captions are Ink, not the
  sticker's Chalk-on-Ink, per the pitch's design notes.
- **Layout.** The canvas is 8.5 × 3.5 in. The wrap's left and right edges
  meet at the handle seam. Side A centers at x 2.125 in and side B at x
  6.375 in, so each faces out on its own side of the handle. Each panel
  spans about 2.8 in across and 0.39 to 3.10 in down. That leaves at least
  0.72 in from the seam, 1.45 in between the panels, and 0.38 in from the
  top and bottom edges.
- **Resolution.** Vector, so it scales without loss. The thinnest lines are
  the 0.023 in sesame and rim details.
- **Contrast.** Ink text on Chalk or white is about 16:1. Yolk appears only
  in non-text shapes, each with an Ink edge.
- **Spelling.** The caption source text is `W chews` and `L nibbles`.
