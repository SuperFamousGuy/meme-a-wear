## Design notes

One original round black cat with the meme's pear-shaped body, no neck, puffy cheeks, huge glossy
eyes, flat pursed mouth, and blank stare. The caption follows the brief's `remix_guidance.lands` for
self-directed laziness, and it suits a hoodie in October. It's a solo piece. The couple angle lives in
the tee and mug.

- **Light variant:** Ink cat with Smoke fur lines, Yolk irises, Ink caption. Ink on light garments
  exceeds 4.5:1. The Chalk background is removable.
- **Dark variant:** recolored from the approved light job, so both variants show the same cat. Chalk
  caption on Ink, about 16:1. The cat keeps an Ink fill with a Chalk outline. The printer needs a
  white underbase. Knock out the Ink background and let the garment show through.
- Keep the print clear of the pouch pocket and drawstrings. The art is square and fills the
  12 x 12 in area.
