## Pitch

**Product.** A crew-neck t-shirt with a front-center print.

**Caption.** "FRUTIGER AERO YAMS" above the yam, and "TO YOUR RIGHT" with a
right-pointing arrow below it. The brief's remix_guidance names the phrase
with an arrow as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** One literal yam, drawn large and tilted on a diagonal. It
keeps the brief's traits: a long, tapered, slightly curved root shape as a
smooth cartoon; glossy saturated sky blue that lightens toward the bottom,
with a thin brighter blue outline; a starburst lens flare on the top edge;
and short pale streaks across the skin. It changes the still's grey
background to none, and adds two or three small glossy bubbles. The arrow is
chunky and glossy to match.

**Buyer.** Ages 13 to 25 who follow brainrot slang and Y2K nostalgia. Others
read it as a Y2K aesthetic shirt.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with high confidence, verified
2026-11-09. KYM, Pop Creep, and an X trending summary covered it within the
past week, with no brand use yet.

**Revised 2026-11-09.** Dropped "Frutiger" and "frutiger aero" from the
listing title and tags. Marketing flagged both as US marks. The caption stays.

## Design notes

- Yam colors: a sky blue gloss built from two or three flat tones plus one
  smooth interior gradient. The artist picks the blues; record them in
  `colors`. The flare, streaks, and bubble highlights are Chalk, not pure
  white.
- Keep gradients inside the yam. Its outer edge is a solid bright blue
  outline, so the printer gets a clean edge with no fine gradient.
- Light garments (white, ash): caption in Ink, about 16:1 on Chalk.
- Dark garments (black, navy): caption in Chalk. The blue yam needs a white
  underbase; its bright outline keeps the edge readable on dark fabric.
- The arrow points to the wearer's right side as the viewer sees it. Check
  that the words "TO YOUR RIGHT" and the arrow direction agree.
- Set the caption in Archivo Black, all caps, eight words or fewer.
