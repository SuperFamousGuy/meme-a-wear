## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in die-cut vinyl sticker.

**Caption.** "HUSS HUSS HUSS", stacked and stepped down in three lines to
the right of the figure, with short motion lines. The brief names "huss
huss huss" as a motion-line gag on gym and basketball items.

**Art direction.** Keep: a blocky figure with a square, pale head and
rectangular limbs; two small black oval eyes and a thin curved smile; a
beanie and hoodie with contrasting drawstrings; a ball at hip height,
caught mid-bounce. Change: Ink outfit with Yolk drawstrings, no star, no
chest text, Yolk ball with Ink seams. Flat vector, thick outline for the
cut.

**Buyer.** Players and gym-goers aged 12 to 25. Stickers suit the young
audience's budget and go on bottles, laptops, and gym bags.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking. A sticker is cheap to list now and
cheap to drop if the audio fades.

## Design notes

- Add a Chalk die-cut border at least 0.1 in wide and 0.125 in bleed.
- Ink text on the Chalk border area, about 16:1. Yolk only for the ball and
  drawstrings.
- Generate at 1:1 on a solid background.
