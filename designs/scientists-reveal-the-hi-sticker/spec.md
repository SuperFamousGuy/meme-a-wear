## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in kiss-cut vinyl sticker shaped like a news thumbnail.

**Caption.** "Scientists reveal the" in a plain serif along the bottom, with a
rounded box holding "Hi" pasted over the last word. This is the original
meme, unchanged. The tee and mug pitches cover the garment and the "coffee"
swap; the sticker is the cheap, faithful version.

**Art direction.** The cat's face fills the frame, close up and head-on. It
keeps the brief's traits: huge round glossy black pupils that fill almost all
of each eye; a closed neutral mouth under a small nose; long whiskers that
spread past the face; the serif headline along the bottom edge; and the box
that covers the end of the headline so the last word looks cut off. Flat
vector, with a flat Ink panel behind the cat in place of the photo blur.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans aged 13 to 30. A low-cost entry and an add-on to the
tee or mug.

**Timing.** The meme placed second in KYM's October 2026 Meme of the Month
poll (2026-11-02). A sticker lists fast and needs no large run.

## Design notes

- Panel is Ink. Fur is Yolk with Ink stripes; Smoke only for whisker and fur
  detail inside Ink areas. Headline is Chalk serif on Ink, about 16:1.
- The "Hi" box is Chalk with Ink text, about 16:1. Let it run off the right
  edge of the headline, as in the still.
- Add 0.125 in bleed. Keep the headline and box at least 0.125 in inside the
  cut line.
- At 3 in, the headline is small. Keep it to the meme's three words and set
  it large enough to read at arm's length.

### Artwork (sticker.svg)

- **Source.** Hand-written SVG fallback. Higgsfield had no image credits on
  2026-11-02. See `open_risks`.
- **Traits kept.** The orange tabby's face fills the frame, head-on and close
  up. Huge round Ink pupils fill almost all of each eye, inside a thin Yolk
  iris ring, with a Chalk catchlight and a small Chalk glint for gloss. A
  small Chalk nose with an Ink outline sits over a closed neutral mouth. Four
  long Chalk whiskers on each side spread past the face onto the panel. The
  Chalk serif headline "Scientists reveal the" runs along the bottom. A
  Chalk rounded box with Ink "Hi" covers the start of the next word, so only
  part of an "s" shows, as in the still.
- **Changes from the still, per the pitch.** Flat Ink panel instead of the
  photo blur. Yolk fur with Ink tabby stripes instead of orange. Chalk nose
  instead of pink. "Hi" in Archivo Black, mixed case as in the meme.
- **Sibling match.** The cat follows the tee and mug design: pointed ears
  with Ink tufts, forehead and cheek stripes, Chalk muzzle and chin, and a
  Chalk outline around the cat (0.05 in), as on the dark tee. The sibling
  tee's dark.png shows the box as "HI" in caps. This sticker keeps the
  spec's and brief's "Hi".
- **Layout.** The canvas is 3.25 × 3.25 in: a 3 in trim plus 0.125 in bleed
  on each side. The Ink panel fills the bleed. The `CutContour` layer is a
  rounded rectangle at the trim, with a 0.15 in corner radius. All art,
  including the headline and box, sits at least 0.125 in inside it. Two
  right whiskers end under the box, which reads as pasted on. `CutContour`
  is a non-printing magenta hairline. Hide it before printing, or map it to
  the printer's cut spot color.
- **Resolution.** Vector, so it scales without loss. The thinnest lines are
  the whiskers, at 0.018 in.
- **Contrast.** Chalk headline on Ink and Ink "Hi" on Chalk are both about
  16:1. Yolk appears only as fur and iris rings, never as text. No Smoke.
- **Spelling.** The source text is `Scientists reveal the`, a cut-off `s`,
  and `Hi`.
- **Size.** The headline is 0.2 in type (about 14 pt), stretched to 2.08 in
  wide. "Hi" is 0.33 in type.
