## Design notes

**Product choice.** A t-shirt. The audience is young and very online, and
remix_guidance frames the image as the wearer's own unhinged mood, which
works best worn. The tall diagonal figure fits a 3:4 front print. A mug
wrap's 8.5 x 3.5 in strip would squash the stretch or split it at the
handle. A sticker is a good follow-up product from the same art.

**What it shows.** An original yellow blob creature stretches diagonally from
lower right to upper left. Almost all of it is a gaping mouth with a Chalk
tongue and big, separated, square Chalk teeth: two on top and four below.
Two round googly eyes, squeezed together, sit at the top tip. Short motion
lines show that it sings. The caption "TRA LA LA" sits below in heavy,
slightly bouncy all caps.

**Visual signature.** The design keeps the brief's first three traits: the
diagonal, lumpy body that is mostly mouth; the gaping mouth with a tongue
and separated square teeth at the top and bottom; and two round eyes close
together at the top. It changes these on purpose:

- The character is an original blob, not SpongeBob, as the brief's `ip`
  field and remix_guidance require.
- The eyes have Ink pupils with no blue irises and no lashes, because those
  are SpongeBob identifiers and blue is outside the brand palette.
- The tongue is Chalk, not pink, to stay in the brand palette.
- Trait four (a flat fan drawing over a show background) is dropped. The
  show background is protected art, and print needs an isolated figure. The
  crude, wobbly line keeps the fan-drawn feel.

**Why the caption fits.** remix_guidance names "tra la la" lettering on an
original warped, stretched-mouth character as the pattern that lands, and it
warns that the joke must read without audio. The words carry the song, and
the mouth carries the volume. The caption is three words, inside the brand's
limit, and states the absurd thing flatly.

**Light variant.** Ink line work and an Ink caption on a flat Chalk
background. The body is Yolk, and the mouth interior is solid Ink. Caption
contrast: Ink on white is about 18:1, on ash about 14:1, on sand above 10:1.
Yolk appears only as a body fill, never as text.

**Dark variant.** The blob has Ink inner lines and a Chalk outer outline on a
flat Ink background. The mouth interior is the Ink background, inside a
Chalk rim. The caption is Chalk: about 16:1 on black and above 12:1 on navy
and charcoal.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the flat background from each PNG: Chalk on `light.png`, Ink on
  `dark.png`. On `light.png`, keep the Chalk fill in the eyes, teeth, and
  tongue as printed Chalk on ash and sand garments.
- On `light.png`, print the mouth interior as solid Ink.
- On `dark.png`, the mouth interior can be bare fabric on black. Print it as
  Ink on navy and charcoal so the mouth stays dark.
- On `dark.png`, print the Ink lines inside the Yolk and Chalk shapes as
  Ink, not as bare fabric.
- DTG on dark garments needs a white underbase under all printed shapes.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 12 x 16 in.
