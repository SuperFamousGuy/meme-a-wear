## Pitch

**Product.** A crew-neck t-shirt with a front-center print, light and dark
variants.

**Caption.** "EVERYBODY GOES OUT PARTYING EXCEPT ME" above the figure, and a
small "huss huss huss" with motion lines beside the ball. The brief's
remix_guidance names the line as a slogan with a dribbling figure, and
"huss huss huss" as a motion-line gag.

**Art direction.** Keep: a blocky figure with a square, pale head and
rectangular torso, arms, and legs; two small black oval eyes and a thin
curved smile; a royal-blue beanie, hoodie, and pants with Yolk
drawstrings; a ball dribbled at hip height. Change: no star, no chest
text, Yolk ball with Ink seams. Flat vector.

**Buyer.** Ballers, gym-goers, and gamers aged 14 to 25 who post the audio.
The joke flatters the lone grinder.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking, with 36 million views in six days
and explainers from 2026-11-19.

**2026-11-24 revision.** Restored the royal-blue outfit, per marketing's
REVISE action, so fans read the figure as the meme. The star and chest
text stay out.

## Design notes

- Outfit color: Huss Blue `#2F5BD3`, a flat fill for the beanie, hoodie,
  and pants only. It's a palette exception pending the user's approval.
  Never use it for text.
- Light garments: Ink caption, Huss Blue outfit with an Ink outline, Chalk
  head with an Ink outline.
- Dark garments: Chalk caption, Chalk head, Huss Blue outfit with a Chalk
  outline at least 0.12 in thick, so it doesn't sink into navy or black
  fabric. DTG needs a white underbase.
- Yolk fills only the ball and drawstrings, never text on light garments.
