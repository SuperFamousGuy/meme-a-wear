## Pitch

**Product.** A pullover hoodie with a front-center print above the pocket.

**Caption.** "*PAWS AT YOU*" above the art and "HOP ON" below it. The brief's
remix_guidance lists "*paws at you* hop on" as a pattern that lands. The
asterisks stay.

**Art direction.** A plush-cute snow leopard sits upright, facing straight
on. It keeps the brief's traits: one front paw pushed toward the viewer,
drawn oversized in the foreground with four round toe pads and a big main
pad; huge round glossy dark eyes, far bigger than a real leopard's; a small
nose and closed neutral mouth low on a short face; thin whiskers; small spots
on the head and larger blotches on the chest and raised leg. It changes the
photo to flat vector art with no background.

**Buyer.** Gamers aged 15 to 30 who send raid and match invites. They wear
the invite instead of texting it.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking: four platforms and a brand post
within a week of 2026-10-23. Hoodie season matches.

## Design notes

- Fur is Chalk with Smoke shading. Spots, eyes, nose, and paw pads are Ink.
  Yolk appears only as a small catchlight in each eye.
- Light hoodies (white, ash): Chalk fur disappears, so give the leopard an
  Ink outline. Captions in Ink, about 16:1 on Chalk.
- Dark hoodies (black, charcoal): captions in Chalk. Ink pads and spots sit
  inside Chalk fur, so they need no extra outline. Give the outer edge of any
  Ink shape that touches the garment a Chalk outline of at least 0.12 in.
- Keep the paw the largest shape. The brief calls it the most recognizable
  detail.
- Keep all art inside 12 × 12 in and clear of the pocket seam and drawstrings.

### Artwork (light.svg, dark.svg)

- **Source.** Hand-written SVG fallback. Higgsfield had no image credits on
  2026-11-02. See `open_risks`.
- **Consistency with the sticker.** The leopard is the sibling sticker's
  drawing, copied shape for shape and scaled 3.6 times. Only the captions
  and the dark variant's Chalk edge differ.
- **Traits kept.** A pale leopard sits upright and faces straight on. One
  oversized front paw pushes at the viewer, with four round Ink toe pads and
  a large Ink main pad. The paw is the largest single shape. The eyes are
  huge, round, glossy Ink circles with a Smoke rim, one Yolk catchlight, and
  a small Chalk glint. A small Ink nose and a closed neutral mouth sit low on
  a short face, with thin Smoke whiskers. Small Ink spots cover the head.
  Larger Ink blotches cover the chest and the raised leg.
- **Changes from the still, per the pitch.** Flat vector, no background. The
  caption is "*PAWS AT YOU*" in Archivo Black caps above the leopard, with
  the asterisks, plus "HOP ON" below it. The nose is Ink instead of grey.
  The whiskers are Smoke instead of white. The paw overlaps the chin. In the
  still, it sits at chest level below the chin. The body ends in a flat edge
  at the bottom, like a bust.
- **Layout.** The canvas is the 12 × 12 in print area, with no background.
  "*PAWS AT YOU*" sits at the top, 10.8 in wide, with its baseline 1.4 in
  from the top edge. The leopard spans about 5.7 × 7.9 in, from 1.8 in to
  9.7 in down. "HOP ON" sits at the bottom, 6.6 in wide, with its baseline
  0.6 in above the bottom edge. Place the bottom edge of the print area
  above the pocket seam and clear of the drawstrings.
- **Dark garments.** A Chalk silhouette under the ears, head, and body gives
  the leopard's outer Ink outline a Chalk edge 0.14 in wide, above the brand
  minimum of 0.12 in. All other Ink shapes sit inside Chalk fur. DTG needs a
  white underbase under every non-Ink shape. The art has no gradients.
- **Resolution.** Vector, so it scales without loss. The thinnest lines are
  the whiskers, at 0.043 in. The Ink outline is 0.079 in.
- **Contrast.** Ink captions on white or ash hoodies are at least 12:1.
  Chalk captions on black or charcoal hoodies are at least 10:1. Yolk
  appears only as the eye catchlights, inside Ink. No text uses Smoke or
  Yolk.
- **Spelling.** The caption source text is `*PAWS AT YOU*` and `HOP ON`.
