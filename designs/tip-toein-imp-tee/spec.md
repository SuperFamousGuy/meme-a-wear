## Design notes

The front shows the imp mid-tiptoe with "TIP TOEIN'" below him. This follows
the brief's remix_guidance: an original imp in the arms-out tiptoe, with
"tip toein'" as the text. The caption was checked letter by letter:
T-I-P T-O-E-I-N-'.

Light variant: the Chalk (#F2F2EE) background is a solid fill for the printer
to remove. The caption is Ink on light garments, about 16:1 contrast. The skin
is a very pale cream near Chalk. Keep it as a fill, not a knockout, so it
doesn't take the shirt color on ash or sand.

Dark variant: generated by recoloring the approved light art (light job as the
only reference), so both variants show the same drawing. Chalk caption on Ink is about 16:1. Ink shapes have a
Chalk outline, but it is thinner than the brand's 0.12 in minimum (see
open_risks). DTG needs a white
underbase. The velvet sheen in Smoke is flat-filled, with no gradients.
