## Pitch

**Product.** A 3 × 3 in kiss-cut vinyl sticker, cut to the yam's contour.

**Caption.** None. The brief's remix_guidance names the literal glossy yam as
a standalone graphic as a pattern that lands. No text also keeps the
trademarked word off the product.

**Art direction.** One literal yam on a diagonal, filling the sticker. It
keeps the brief's traits: a long, tapered, slightly curved root shape as a
smooth cartoon; glossy saturated sky blue that lightens toward the bottom,
with a thin brighter blue outline; a starburst lens flare on the top edge;
and short pale streaks across the skin. It drops the still's grey
background and adds two small glossy bubbles beside the yam.

**Buyer.** Ages 13 to 25 who follow brainrot slang and Y2K nostalgia, for
laptops, phone cases, and water bottles. A low-cost add-on to the tee.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with high confidence, verified
2026-11-09. Stickers list fast while the slang is new.

**Revised 2026-11-09.** Dropped "Frutiger" and "frutiger aero" from the
listing title and tags, which marketing flagged as US marks. Switched from
die-cut to kiss-cut, which keeps the margin positive.

## Design notes

- Kiss-cut along the contour of the yam and bubbles, with a Chalk border at
  least 0.125 in wide. The backing stays square. Add 0.125 in bleed past the
  cut line.
- Keep gradients inside the yam. Its outer edge is a solid bright blue
  outline, so the contour cut follows a clean edge.
- The flare and streaks are Chalk, not pure white. The flare's points may
  reach the border but must not cross the cut line.
- One variant: `sticker.png`.
