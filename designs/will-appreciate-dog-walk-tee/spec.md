## Pitch

**Product.** A crew-neck t-shirt with a chest print, light and dark variants.

**Caption.** Title: "TAKE THE DOG OUT". Tag line: "+♥ The dog will
appreciate this". Instruction: "Bring the bags this time." The brief lists
the name swap "The dog will appreciate this" as a pattern that lands.

**Art direction.** Keep the brief's traits: a dark rectangular box, a thin
colored top line, a bold condensed all-caps title, a small "+" and solid
heart before a rounded sans tag line, and one sentence-case instruction.
Change: Ink box, Yolk accents, no portrait square. Flat vector.

**Buyer.** Dog owners aged 16 to 35 who play games or follow meme accounts.
The name swap also sells to people who never saw the game. A gift from the
partner who always walks the dog.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with high confidence. The dog swap
keeps the shirt funny after the game meme cools.

## Design notes

- The box is a solid Ink fill on both variants, so the text is always Chalk
  on Ink, about 16:1. Yolk fills the top line, "+", and heart.
- Dark garments: give the box a Chalk outline at least 0.12 in thick. DTG
  needs a white underbase under all Chalk and Yolk.
- Light garments: the Ink box needs no outline.
- Generate at about 2:1 on a solid background, then center it on the chest.
