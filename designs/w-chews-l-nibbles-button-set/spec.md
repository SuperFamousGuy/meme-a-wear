## Pitch

**Product.** Two 3 x 3 in kiss-cut stickers, sold together as a set.

**Caption.** Sticker one says "W chews". Sticker two says "L nibbles". The
brief's remix_guidance names a two-sided rating as a pattern that lands; a
pair lets the buyer stick a verdict where it belongs.

**Art direction.** Each sticker shows the phrase in plain bold sans, as the
brief's traits describe: a capital W or L, the rest lowercase. Under "W
chews", a small heaped rice bowl with a glossy glaze. Under "L nibbles", the
same bowl with one sad piece left. The bowl comes from the meal in the brief's
still, redrawn as a simple flat icon. W uses a dark face, L a light face, so
the pair reads as opposites at a glance.

**Buyer.** Students and food posters aged 13 to 25 who use W and L slang and
decorate laptops, bottles, and lunch boxes. A low-cost add-on to the mug.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging, re-verified 2026-11-02. Stickers
list fast while the slang is new.

## Design notes

- "W chews" sticker: Ink face, Chalk text, Yolk glaze. Chalk on Ink is about
  16:1.
- "L nibbles" sticker: Chalk face, Ink text and bowl. No Yolk text on Chalk.
- Each sticker is a rounded square or circle, 3 x 3 in, with 0.125 in bleed
  past the cut line. Keep text and bowl at least 0.125 in inside the cut line
  so a slight cut shift doesn't clip them.
- Both stickers use the same bowl drawing, size, and text position, so they
  read as a matched pair.
- Variants: one file per sticker face, `w.png` and `l.png`.

### Artwork (w.svg, l.svg)

- **Source.** Hand-written SVG fallback, so the files are `w.svg` and
  `l.svg`, not PNG. Higgsfield had no image credits on 2026-11-02. See
  `open_risks`.
- **Traits kept.** The phrases "W chews" and "L nibbles", with a capital W
  or L and the rest lowercase, in plain bold sans above a meal. The meal is
  the still's bowl: a white bowl heaped with glazed, sesame-topped chunks
  over rice.
- **Changes from the still, per the pitch.** The "W chews or L nibbles"
  choice splits into two matched stickers. The meal is a flat icon. The
  glaze is Yolk, the sesame is Ink, and the scallions are left out to keep
  the three-color palette. On "W chews", ten chunks heap above a Chalk bowl
  on Ink, with two Yolk glaze drips over the rim. On "L nibbles", the same
  bowl is Ink on Chalk, empty except for one small tilted chunk and three
  loose seeds.
- **Layout.** The canvas is 3.25 × 3.25 in: a 3 in trim plus 0.125 in bleed
  on each side. The `CutContour` layer is the kiss-cut line, a rounded
  square with 0.3 in corners. It's a non-printing magenta hairline. Hide it
  before printing, or map it to the printer's cut spot color. The caption,
  bowl, and rim sit in the same place on both stickers. All art sits at
  least 0.125 in inside the cut line, which leaves a safe area from 0.25 to
  3.0 in on each axis. The caption spans 0.55 to 2.7 in across. The bowl
  spans 0.85 to 2.4 in across, and the heap and foot span 1.33 to 2.72 in
  down.
- **Resolution.** Vector, so it scales without loss. The thinnest lines are
  the 0.018 in rim lines.
- **Contrast.** Chalk text on Ink and Ink text on Chalk are about 16:1. Yolk
  appears only in non-text shapes. On Chalk, every Yolk shape has an Ink
  edge.
- **Spelling.** The caption source text is `W chews` and `L nibbles`.
