## Pitch

**Product.** Two 3 x 3 in kiss-cut stickers, sold together as a set.

**Caption.** Sticker one says "W chews". Sticker two says "L nibbles". The
brief's remix_guidance names a two-sided rating as a pattern that lands; a
pair lets the buyer stick a verdict where it belongs.

**Art direction.** Each sticker shows the phrase in plain bold sans, as the
brief's traits describe: a capital W or L, the rest lowercase. Under "W
chews", a small heaped rice bowl with a glossy glaze. Under "L nibbles", the
same bowl with one sad piece left. The bowl comes from the meal in the brief's
still, redrawn as a simple flat icon. W uses a dark face, L a light face, so
the pair reads as opposites at a glance.

**Buyer.** Students and food posters aged 13 to 25 who use W and L slang and
decorate laptops, bottles, and lunch boxes. A low-cost add-on to the mug.

**Timing.** The brief rates it emerging, re-verified 2026-11-02. Stickers
list fast while the slang is new.

## Design notes

- "W chews" sticker: Ink face, Chalk text, Yolk glaze. Chalk on Ink is about
  16:1.
- "L nibbles" sticker: Chalk face, Ink text and bowl. No Yolk text on Chalk.
- Each sticker is a rounded square or circle, 3 x 3 in, with 0.125 in bleed
  past the cut line. Keep text and bowl at least 0.125 in inside the cut line
  so a slight cut shift doesn't clip them.
- Both stickers use the same bowl drawing, size, and text position, so they
  read as a matched pair.
- Variants: one file per sticker face, `w.png` and `l.png`.
