## Design notes

**Product choice.** A t-shirt. The brief's audience is teens and young
adults in alt and indie circles, and "losercore" works as a label you wear.
The vertical stack of figures and word fits a 3:4 front print.

**What it shows.** Three restroom-sign pictograms stand on one baseline. On
the left, a hollow man and woman hold hands under a small Yolk heart. On the
right, past a wide gap, a solid figure stands alone with arms at its sides.
"LOSERCORE" sits below in heavy, pixel-stepped all caps.

**Why it fits remix_guidance.** The brief lists two patterns that land: the
word "losercore" as a self-deprecating label in lo-fi or early-2000s type,
and an original pictogram gag with a couple and heart beside a lone figure.
This design uses both. The solid lone figure is the wearer, the only figure
drawn with full weight. The word labels the mood without explaining it. The
design uses nothing from the AI couple, the album-cover palette, or the
playlist, so it can't read as an ad for the playlist.

**Visual signature.** The design keeps trait four (a couple with a heart
beside a lone figure, drawn as pictograms). It drops traits one to three,
the blue-and-pink AI couple and its low-poly rendering, on purpose. The
brief's ip field and falls_flat guidance rule them out.

**Light variant.** Ink outlines for the couple, a solid Ink lone figure, a
Yolk heart, and an Ink caption on a flat Chalk background. Caption contrast:
Ink on white is about 18:1, on ash about 14:1, and on sand above 10:1. Yolk
appears only as the heart, never as text.

**Dark variant.** Chalk outlines for the couple, a solid Chalk lone figure, a
Yolk heart, and a Chalk caption on a flat Ink background. Caption contrast:
Chalk on black is about 16:1, and above 12:1 on navy and charcoal. The art
has no Ink shapes that need a Chalk outline.

**Printer notes.**

- Remove the flat background from each PNG: Chalk on `light.png`, Ink on
  `dark.png`.
- The couple's insides are hollow. Print them as bare fabric on every
  garment.
- DTG on dark garments needs a white underbase under all printed shapes.
- Keep the thin arm slits on the lone figure open. Widen them slightly in
  vector cleanup if needed.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 12 x 16 in.
