## Pitch

**Product.** A front-print t-shirt in light and dark variants.

**Caption.** "Scientists reveal the" in a plain serif along the bottom, with
a rounded box holding "HI" pasted over the last word.

**Art direction.** The design keeps every trait in the brief: the ginger
cat's face fills the frame, head-on and close up. Huge glossy black pupils
fill almost all of each eye. The mouth is closed and neutral under a small
nose, and long whiskers spread past the face. The serif headline runs along
the bottom, and the "HI" box covers its last word. The design changes the
photo into flat vector art in the brand palette. It drops the photo's blurry
background and the outlet's styling.

**Buyer.** Ages 13 to 30 who share cat memes on Instagram and TikTok. It's
a wholesome joke that works as a greeting. People wear it to say hi without
saying hi.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with medium confidence. KYM's entry
(2026-10-15) and roundup (2026-10-23) put the spike in the last two weeks.
The brief found no brand use or "overdone" posts yet.

## Design notes

- **Light variant** (white, ash, sand): Yolk fur, Ink stripes and outlines,
  Ink pupils with Chalk glints, Ink serif headline. The "HI" box is Chalk with
  an Ink outline and Ink letters.
- **Dark variant** (black, navy, charcoal): same art with Chalk outlines at
  least 0.12 in thick, a Chalk serif headline, and a Chalk box with Ink "HI".
- Contrast: Ink on light garments and Chalk on dark garments are about 16:1.
  Yolk never carries text.
- Generate at 3:4 to match the 12 x 16 in print area. The headline must stay
  inside the bottom edge of the print area.
