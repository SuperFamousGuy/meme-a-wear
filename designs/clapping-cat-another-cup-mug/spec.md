## Design notes

**Concept.** The cat applauds a small, harmless overindulgence: one more cup
of coffee. The brief's `remix_guidance` lists late-night snacks and small bad
decisions as captions that work. A refill is the mug-native version of that
joke. The caption is the drinker's question, and the cat's applause answers
it, so the joke works in one still image. The caption is original.

**How it differs from `clapping-cat-bad-idea-tee`.** That design is a
t-shirt with a centered cat over a generic "BAD IDEA. LOVE IT." caption. This
one is a mug wrap with a side-by-side layout: the cat and a coffee cup on the
left half, the caption on the right half. It's tied to a specific, everyday
act.

**Layout.** On an 11 oz mug, the handle covers the seam where the left and
right edges of the wrap meet. The prompt asks for empty margins at both
edges, so the cat and caption sit on opposite faces of the mug, clear of the
handle. A right-handed drinker sees the cat. The person across the table sees
the caption. Keep key content 0.25 in from every edge.

**Colors and contrast.** The caption is Ink (`#141414`) on Chalk (`#F2F2EE`),
about 16:1. On bare white ceramic, it's higher. The caption doesn't use Yolk,
because Yolk is under 2:1 on light surfaces. Yolk appears only in the eyes and
the coffee surface. Smoke appears only as fur lines and whiskers, never text.

**Printer notes.**

- Sublimation can't print white. The Chalk background prints as a very light
  tint on a white mug. The printer may remove the background and let bare
  white ceramic show. The teeth then show as mug white, which reads the same.
- No printer template was used. The print area follows the `design-spec`
  default of 8.5 x 3.5 in for an 11 oz wrap.
