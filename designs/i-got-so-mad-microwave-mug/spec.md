## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with the same panel on each side of the handle.

**Caption.** "I GOT SO MAD I MICROWAVED IT AGAIN" (seven words). The brief's
remix_guidance names niche petty lines that fit the product, such as a
coffee line on a mug, plus a drawing of a snapped object under the caption.

**Art direction.** Keep: a caption that starts "I got so mad I", centered
above the aftermath, and a broken everyday object seen from above on a flat
surface. The object is a wooden coffee stir stick snapped in two, with a few
splinters, on a single Ink table line. Change: Ink text with no outline, in
caps. Flat vector, no wood-grain photo.

**Buyer.** Office workers and students aged 16 to 30 who reheat the same
coffee three times. A cheap desk gift.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking, with near-million-like compilations
and KYM coverage through 2026-11-20. A text-led mug can list quickly.

## Design notes

- Prints on white ceramic. The Chalk background is removable.
- Ink text and outlines, about 16:1 on white. Yolk fills the stick wood,
  never text.
- Keep content 0.25 in from each edge and clear of the handle seam.
- Generate one panel at about 1:1, then place it on both sides of the wrap.
