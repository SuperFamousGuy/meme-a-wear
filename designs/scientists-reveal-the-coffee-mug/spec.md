## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with a full-wrap print.

**Caption.** "Scientists reveal the" in a plain serif, with a rounded box
holding "COFFEE" pasted over the last word. The brief's remix_guidance names
this exact swap as a pattern that lands on a mug.

**Art direction.** The cat's face sits in the center of the wrap, on the
side that faces away from the handle. It keeps the brief's traits: head-on
and close up, huge glossy black pupils, a closed neutral mouth under a small
nose, and long whiskers that spread toward the sides of the mug. The headline
and the box run along the bottom of the wrap. The art is flat vector in the
brand palette, with no photo background.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans who want the joke at their desk. It also works as a
gift for anyone who won't talk before coffee.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with medium confidence. Coverage
spiked from 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-23. A mug is the swap the brief itself
suggests, so it extends the trend to a second product type.

## Design notes

- The wrap prints on white ceramic. The Chalk background is removable, so
  Chalk areas print as bare white.
- Ink headline and Ink "COFFEE" on a Chalk box: about 16:1. Yolk never
  carries text.
- Keep the face, headline, and box at least 0.25 in from each edge and clear
  of the handle seam. Generate at 21:9 and pad the sides with Chalk.
