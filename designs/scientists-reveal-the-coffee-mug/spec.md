## Pitch

**Product.** An 11 oz mug with a full-wrap print.

**Caption.** "Scientists reveal the" in a plain serif, with a rounded box
holding "COFFEE" pasted over the last word. The brief's remix_guidance names
this exact swap as a pattern that lands on a mug.

**Art direction.** The cat's face sits in the center of the wrap, on the
side that faces away from the handle. It keeps the brief's traits: head-on
and close up, huge glossy black pupils, a closed neutral mouth under a small
nose, and long whiskers that spread toward the sides of the mug. The headline
and the box run along the bottom of the wrap. The art is flat vector in the
brand palette, with no photo background.

**Buyer.** Cat-meme fans who want the joke at their desk. It also works as a
gift for anyone who won't talk before coffee.

**Timing.** The brief rates it peaking with medium confidence. Coverage
spiked from 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-23. A mug is the swap the brief itself
suggests, so it extends the trend to a second product type.

## Design notes

- The wrap prints on white ceramic. The Chalk background is removable, so
  Chalk areas print as bare white.
- Ink headline and Ink "COFFEE" on a Chalk box: about 16:1. Yolk never
  carries text.
- Keep the face, headline, and box at least 0.25 in from each edge and clear
  of the handle seam. Generate at 21:9 and pad the sides with Chalk.

### Artwork (artist, 2026-11-03)

- **Traits kept.** wrap.png shows every trait from the brief, with the

  Ink pupils with a thin Yolk rim and a Chalk glint; a small Chalk nose over
  a closed, neutral mouth and a pale muzzle; long whiskers spreading toward

- **Same cat as the tee.** The prompt passed the tee's approved light.png
  as the character reference, so the mug and tee show the same cat.
- **Layout.** The face is centered, so it faces away from a handle at the
  wrap seam. The whisker tips stop about 8 percent from each side, clear of
  the handle. The top and bottom margins are too tight; see `open_risks`.
- **Attempts.** A 4k submit was rejected for lack of credits and created no
  job. The 2k submit (job 69d86839-db09-4bcc-81de-67b70934b666) passed
  review on the first attempt, apart from the margins. The margin edit pass
  and the mockup were then rejected for lack of credits.
- **Resolution.** 2688 x 1152 px on 8.5 x 3.5 in is about 316 DPI. Placed
  at 82 percent for the margin fix without resampling, it prints at about
  386 DPI. No upscale needed.
- **Contrast.** Ink text on the white mug and Ink "COFFEE" on the Chalk box
  are about 16:1. Yolk carries no text.
- **Spelling.** Every letter checked: "Scientists reveal the" and "COFFEE".
- **Text is generated.** The headline serif and the "COFFEE" sans are not
  font files. The caption is not Archivo Black; see the brand-conflict open
  risk.
- **Not SVG.** wrap.png is a Higgsfield image. This design has no SVG
  fallback.
