# Target market

This profile describes who buys meme-a-wear products. It draws on the
`audience`, `platforms`, and `tone` fields of the eight trend briefs behind
reviewed designs, and on the 23 viability reports in `marketing/viability/`.
The store has no sales data yet, so every claim is a research estimate.

Confidence: `low` means one source, `medium` means two that agree, and `high`
means three or more that agree. `contested` means sources conflict.

All eight briefs were last verified 2026-10-18T03:44:00Z. They become stale
after 2026-10-21T03:44:00Z.

## Claims across trends

### Age

- **The core buyer is 16 to 25.** Every brief's age range covers 16 to 25.
  - sources: black-cat-clapping (13–30), randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah (13–35),
    tra-la-la-spongebob (12–25), fat-talking-tom-and-angela (13–25),
    biddy-blas-scrib-doon (16–40), ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein (16–35),
    youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan (16–40), losercore-playlist (15–28)
  - confidence: high
- **The upper age bound is contested.**
  - Side A, tops out at 25 to 30: tra-la-la-spongebob, fat-talking-tom-and-angela,
    losercore-playlist, black-cat-clapping.
  - Side B, reaches 35 to 40: randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah,
    ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein, biddy-blas-scrib-doon,
    youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan.
  - confidence: contested. The split follows the trend, so read the trend
    segment below for each product.
- **Part of the audience is under 16, and may not buy on Etsy directly.**
  Products for these trends may depend on parents or gift buyers.
  - sources: tra-la-la-spongebob (12+), black-cat-clapping (13+),
    randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah (13+), fat-talking-tom-and-angela (13+),
    viability/tra-la-la-blob-tee
  - confidence: high

### Platforms

- **TikTok is the main platform.** All eight briefs list it.
  - sources: all eight briefs
  - confidence: high
- **X carries most of these memes too.**
  - sources: black-cat-clapping, tra-la-la-spongebob,
    fat-talking-tom-and-angela, biddy-blas-scrib-doon,
    ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein, youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan,
    losercore-playlist
  - confidence: high
- **Instagram (Reels) reaches the audience for five trends.**
  - sources: black-cat-clapping, randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah,
    biddy-blas-scrib-doon, ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein,
    youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan
  - confidence: high
- **YouTube carries five of the trends.** meme-a-wear doesn't use it as a
  launch channel.
  - sources: randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah, tra-la-la-spongebob,
    biddy-blas-scrib-doon, ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein, losercore-playlist
  - confidence: high
- **No brief puts the audience on Pinterest.** Pinterest suits desk and mug
  posts in some reports, but no brief gives audience evidence for it.
  - sources: all eight briefs (absence); viability/whats-up-monday-mug,
    viability/tip-toein-inbox-mug, viability/chubby-cat-hibernating-hoodie
    (suggest Pinterest as a product fit)
  - confidence: high that the briefs show no Pinterest audience
- **Several memes are audio-led, and a business account can't reuse the
  audio.** Posts for these trends must work without the original sound.
  - sources: viability/tra-la-la-blob-tee, viability/not-me-nan-tee,
    viability/tip-toein-imp-tee, viability/scrib-doon-tee
  - confidence: high

### Tone

- **The humor is ironic, not earnest.** Mock-sincere, mock-hype, trollish,
  and deadpan delivery recur.
  - sources: black-cat-clapping, randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah,
    ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein, youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan
  - confidence: high
- **Absurd and surreal humor lands.**
  - sources: black-cat-clapping, randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah,
    tra-la-la-spongebob
  - confidence: high
- **Self-deprecating humor lands.**
  - sources: losercore-playlist, fat-talking-tom-and-angela
  - confidence: medium
- **Loud, goofy bravado lands.**
  - sources: randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah, biddy-blas-scrib-doon
  - confidence: medium

### Product types

No brief records merch-buying evidence. These claims come from viability
judgments, not sales.

- **They buy t-shirts.**
  - sources: viability/losercore-table-for-one-tee,
    viability/whats-up-brah-alarm-clock-tee, viability/tra-la-la-blob-tee,
    viability/chubby-cat-couple-tee, viability/scrib-doon-tee
  - confidence: high
- **They buy laptop and notebook stickers.**
  - sources: viability/losercore-sad-songs-sticker,
    viability/whats-up-brah-sticker, viability/clapping-cat-great-choice-sticker,
    viability/scrib-doon-sticker
  - confidence: high
- **They buy hoodies, especially cozy, late-night themes in autumn.**
  - sources: viability/clapping-cat-one-more-episode-hoodie,
    viability/chubby-cat-hibernating-hoodie
  - confidence: medium
- **Mug buying is contested.**
  - For: viability/not-me-nan-wolf-mug (tea mug suits a 16–40 UK audience),
    viability/chubby-cat-couple-mug (couple mugs), viability/scrib-doon-mug
    (desk mugs for fans).
  - Against: viability/tra-la-la-7am-mug and viability/losercore-my-date-mug
    (audiences under about 28 buy mugs less than tees or stickers).
  - Unknown: viability/whats-up-monday-mug, viability/tip-toein-inbox-mug.
  - confidence: contested. Mugs look stronger for the older 16–40 segments
    and as gifts.
- **Matching couple items sell to couples.**
  - sources: fat-talking-tom-and-angela, viability/chubby-cat-couple-tee,
    viability/chubby-cat-couple-mug
  - confidence: low. All three rest on one brief.
- **Dog owners buy pet products.**
  - sources: youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan, viability/not-me-nan-dog-bandana
  - confidence: low. The report relies on the same brief.

## Segments by trend

### Black Cat Clapping

- Ages 13–30, mostly on TikTok and Instagram Reels; also X. Sarcastic,
  mock-sincere applause.
  - sources: black-cat-clapping
  - confidence: low
- Shares when a caption names a specific bad decision, then cuts to the cat.
  - sources: black-cat-clapping, viability/clapping-cat-bad-idea-tee,
    viability/clapping-cat-another-cup-mug
  - confidence: low. The reports rely on the same brief.

### Randy Marsh vs. / What's Up Brah?

- Ages 13–35 on TikTok and Reels; also YouTube. South Park fans and gamers.
  Absurd bravado and mock fight-card hype.
  - sources: randy-marsh-vs-whats-up-brah
  - confidence: low
- Shares "mascot vs. X" face-offs that they can stage with the product.
  - sources: viability/whats-up-brah-alarm-clock-tee,
    viability/whats-up-brah-sticker, viability/whats-up-monday-mug
  - confidence: low. The reports rely on the same brief.

### Tra La La SpongeBob

- Ages 12–25 on TikTok and X; also YouTube. Brainrot and animation fans.
  Chaotic, surreal humor.
  - sources: tra-la-la-spongebob
  - confidence: low

### Fat Talking Tom and Angela

- Ages 13–25 on X and TikTok; also Reddit. Couples, plus Talking Tom
  nostalgia. Cozy, self-deprecating laziness.
  - sources: fat-talking-tom-and-angela
  - confidence: low. The brief is `emerging` with low confidence.

### Biddy Blas / Scrib Doon

- Star Wars fans and gamers, ages 16–40, on X and YouTube; the meme also
  spreads on Instagram and TikTok. Affectionate fandom in-joke with loud,
  goofy bravado.
  - sources: biddy-blas-scrib-doon
  - confidence: low
- Shares loud self-introductions set to the line, and "scrib doon" as text
  for "write that down."
  - sources: biddy-blas-scrib-doon
  - confidence: low
- Gift buyers outside the fandom are a weak segment. The phrase means nothing
  out of context.
  - sources: biddy-blas-scrib-doon
  - confidence: low
- Posts can't use the game footage, audio, character, or franchise name.
  - sources: biddy-blas-scrib-doon, viability/scrib-doon-tee
  - confidence: low. The report relies on the same brief.
- The trend has peaked since 2026-10-10 and may fade within weeks unless game
  updates revive it. This is an estimate; the brief gives no fade date.
  - sources: biddy-blas-scrib-doon
  - confidence: low

### AI Rumpelstiltskin "Tip Toein' In My Jordans"

- Broad audience, ages 16–35, on TikTok and X; also Instagram and YouTube.
  Creepy-funny, trollish humor.
  - sources: ai-rumpelstiltskin-tip-toein
  - confidence: low

### You're Blatantly Just Not Me Nan

- UK-leaning, ages 16–40, on TikTok; also X and Instagram. Dog owners.
  Deadpan British understatement.
  - sources: youre-blatantly-just-not-me-nan
  - confidence: low
- Owners share clips of their own dog dressed as Nan.
  - sources: viability/not-me-nan-dog-bandana
  - confidence: low

### Losercore Playlist

- Ages 15–28 on TikTok and X; also YouTube and Spotify. Indie and alt-music
  listeners. Self-deprecating loneliness, mixed with mockery of AI slop.
  - sources: losercore-playlist
  - confidence: low. The brief has low confidence.
