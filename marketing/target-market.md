## Biddy Blas / Scrib Doon

Source: `trends/biddy-blas-scrib-doon.md`, last verified 2026-10-18T03:44:00Z.
The brief is `peaking` with `medium` confidence and `caution` risk.

- **Who.** Star Wars fans and gamers, ages 16-40. They know the Biddy Blas
  clip from Star Wars Galactic Racer, which launched 2026-10-06.
- **Where.** X and YouTube first. The meme also spreads on Instagram, TikTok,
  and YouTube Shorts.
- **Tone they share.** Affectionate fandom in-joke, with loud, goofy bravado.
- **What makes them share.** Loud self-introductions and brags set to the
  line, and "scrib doon" used as text for "write that down."
- **What they buy.** Fan items that signal the in-joke to other fans: tees,
  notebook and laptop stickers, and desk mugs.
- **Who doesn't buy.** Anyone who hasn't seen the clip. The brief says the
  phrase means nothing out of context, so gift buyers outside the fandom are a
  weak segment.
- **Limits on reaching them.** Lucasfilm owns the character, the game, and the
  clip. Posts can't use the game footage, audio, character, or "Star Wars"
  name. That removes the hooks that this audience responds to most.
- **Window.** The trend has peaked since 2026-10-10. Expect it to fade within
  weeks unless game updates revive it. Treat this as an estimate. The brief
  doesn't give a fade date.
