## Audience

- The Scientists Reveal the Hi segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  13 to 30 on Instagram Reels and TikTok, plus cat-meme fans on Reddit.
- Mug buying is `contested` in the profile. Mugs look stronger for older
  buyers and as gifts. This mug is for fans with a desk job or a dorm desk,
  and for people who buy a gift for a friend who won't talk before coffee.
- The audience already shares one-word recaptions of the headline. "Coffee"
  is one more recaption, so a desk photo joins that pattern.

## Channels

Use organic posts only. As of 2026-10-18 the store had no TikTok, Instagram,
or Pinterest account. The user creates each one if it still doesn't exist.

1. **Instagram.** The brief says the spike rests mostly on Instagram
   recaptions. A desk shot of the mug is itself a recaption and needs no
   audio.
2. **TikTok.** The profile rates TikTok the main platform (high). A mug
   reveal clip is short and easy to film. Business accounts are limited to
   the Commercial Music Library.
3. **Pinterest.** No brief puts this audience on Pinterest (profile: high),
   but gift buyers search for funny mugs there. Pin the mockup to a gift
   board. Low priority, but higher than for the tee.
