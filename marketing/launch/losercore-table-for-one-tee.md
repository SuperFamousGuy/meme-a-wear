## Audience

The buyers are the Losercore segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 15
to 28, indie and alt listeners, on TikTok and X (confidence: low, one brief).
They match the core buyer, ages 16 to 25 (high), and they buy tees (high).
The tone is self-deprecating (medium).

They share a joke about themselves. The tee lets the wearer say "I'm the
single one" before anyone else can. That is the Unity principle at work: wearing it marks
you as one of the in-group. Posts should stage the wearer as the solid figure,
never as someone bitter at the couple. The brief warns that incel framing
falls flat.

## Channels

1. **TikTok.** Create an account. All eight briefs, and this one, put the
   audience here (high). The pictogram and one word read in a one-second
   frame. The meme is visual, so posts don't need the original audio. Use a
   business account and only sounds from the Commercial Music Library.
2. **Instagram.** Create an account. Reels can reuse the TikTok clips.
   Carousels suit the flat lay. The profile rates Instagram high across
   trends, but this brief doesn't list it, so it ranks second.
3. **Pinterest.** Optional. No brief puts this audience on Pinterest. Pin the
   mockups only if the account exists for other products. Pins send search
   traffic to Etsy for months, at no cost.
