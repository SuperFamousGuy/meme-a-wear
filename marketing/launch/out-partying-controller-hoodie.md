## Audience

- The While They're Out Partying segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
  ages 13 to 25 on TikTok and Instagram Reels, with a strong gamer crossover.
  The profile rates hoodies a buy (high), especially stay-in themes in autumn.
- Fans under 16 may need a parent or gift buyer (profile: high). A $36.99
  hoodie hits this group hardest, so frame later posts as a gift for a gamer.
- They share self-filmed clips of a niche skill under the caption. They share
  this hoodie to brag about a useless skill with a straight face.

## Posts

All copy follows the brand voice: dry, short, and never explaining the
meme. Never use a frame or audio from the viral clips, and never name or tag
their creators. Don't show a recognizable game, console logo, or branded
controller on screen. Don't use the gym framing that mimics another
gym-goer. The joke mocks the wearer's own skill.

**TikTok concept 1, "the format."** Overlay the caption "While they're out
partying btw" in TikTok's default text. Film a plain controller on a desk at
night, with no hands in frame, a lamp on, and a clock reading 3 a.m. End on the
hoodie folded beside it.
- Caption: `The thumbsticks know what you did.`
- Hashtags: `#whiletheyreoutpartying #btw #gamer #gamerhumor #gaminghoodie`

**TikTok concept 2, "the fail."** The brief notes that parody versions show
the person failing. Same caption, then a "GAME OVER"-style screen drawn by
the store, not taken from a game. Cut to the hoodie.
- Caption: `Very, very good at something. Not this.`

**TikTok concept 3, "the gift."** On-screen text: "for the friend who stayed
in and got very good at something nobody asked about." Hoodie on a chair.
- Caption: `Some people went out.`
