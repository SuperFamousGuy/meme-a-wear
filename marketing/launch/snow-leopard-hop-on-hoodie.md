## Audience

- The Snow Leopard Paws at You segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  15 to 30 on X, Instagram, and TikTok, with a strong gamer core. Gamers are
  a recurring segment (profile confidence: high), and they buy hoodies,
  especially gamer themes in autumn (high).
- They share the caption plus a short request, usually a game invite. The
  hoodie is the invite in wearable form. They share it to tag the friend who
  never hops on.
- Fans under 16 may need a parent or gift buyer (high). At $36.99 this is a
  gift purchase for that group, not an impulse buy.

meme. Never use the zoo photo, the eye photos, any game or store name or
