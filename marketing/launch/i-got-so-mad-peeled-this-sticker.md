## Audience

- The I Got So Mad segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to 25
  on TikTok, plus very-online teens on Instagram and Reddit (low
  confidence). They buy laptop and notebook stickers (high).
- Students fill laptops and bottles with stickers. This one is its own
  punchline: the sticker is the object the rage hit.
- At $4.49 it's an impulse buy, and an add-on for tee buyers.
- A photo of the sticker on a laptop is the meme's own format, so buyers
  can post it as-is.
