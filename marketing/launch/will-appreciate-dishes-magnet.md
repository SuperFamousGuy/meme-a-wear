## Audience

- The Lucia Will Appreciate This segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
  ages 16 to 35, gamers on X and Instagram, plus couples and dating-humor
  accounts (gamers: high; couples: medium).
- The profile has no evidence that this audience buys magnets (low). Aim it
  at gift buyers: the partner, roommate, or parent who always does the
  dishes, and older players near 35.
- Fans share their own task rewrites. A chore mission on the fridge is that
  rewrite as an object, and the "Mom" swap reaches people who never played
  the game (name-swap claim: low).
