## Audience

- The Black Cat Clapping segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  30, on TikTok and Instagram Reels. This mug suits the older end, 18 to 30,
  who drink coffee daily. The profile marks mug buying as contested, and
  stronger for older buyers and gift buyers.
- Gift buyers who know a coffee-dependent fan. Holiday gift searches start
  in November.
- They share when a caption names a specific, harmless bad decision, then
  cuts to the cat (target-market.md, Black Cat Clapping segment). A second
  coffee is that decision.
