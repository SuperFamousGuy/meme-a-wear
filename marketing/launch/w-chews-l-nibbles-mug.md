## Audience

- The W Chews / L Nibbles segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  25 on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and X who already use W and L slang, plus
  food posters (food segment: medium).
- Mug buying is contested for this age group in the profile. Mugs do better
  as gifts and desk items. Pitch the mug to the friend who buys for the
  person who reviews every meal out loud.
- Fans share food photos and clips with a verdict. A mug that turns to W or
  L beside a meal is that verdict as an object.

## Channels

Use organic posts only. As of 2026-10-18 the store had no TikTok, Instagram,
or Pinterest account. The user creates each one if it still doesn't exist.

meme. Never show, name, tag, or reference the reality-TV star from the
