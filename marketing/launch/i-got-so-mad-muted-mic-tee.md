## Audience

- The I Got So Mad segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to 25
  on TikTok, plus gamers and very-online teens on Instagram and Reddit
  (low confidence). Gamers are a core segment across trends (high), and
  t-shirt buying is high.
- The buyer mutes mid-match instead of yelling. The tee states that
  restraint as rage, which is the meme's whole joke.
- They share their own "I got so mad I..." act. The tee gives them one to
  repost, and the comment section invites more.
- Part of the audience is under 16 (profile: high). At $21.99 the tee is a
  gift buy for some of them. Pitch it to friends and siblings, not to
  parents.
