## Audience

- The Snow Leopard Paws at You segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  15 to 30, gamers and cat-meme fans (both segments: high). They buy laptop
  stickers (high).
- The sticker carries only "*paws at you*", with no game request, so it
  reaches cat-meme fans who don't game. It's the cheapest way to own the
  meme, and an add-on for hoodie and mug buyers.
- Fans share it as a stand-in for asking for attention: on a laptop, a
  controller case, or a desk.

## Channels

or Pinterest account. The user creates each one if it still doesn't exist.
