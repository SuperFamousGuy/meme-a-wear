## Audience

- The Frutiger Aero Yams segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  25 who follow brainrot slang (profile: medium) and the Y2K aesthetic (low).
  They buy laptop, bottle, and notebook stickers (high).
- Fans know the literal glossy yam from the viral posts; a 2026-10-28 X post
  of it passed 28,000 likes (brief). The sticker is that image, with no
  text. Fans share it as an inside joke on a laptop or water bottle.
- It's the cheapest way to own the meme, and an add-on for tee buyers.
  Buyers under 16 can afford it without a gift buyer.
- The listing leaves out the meme's name, so Etsy search finds it less
  often. Social posts and the tee listing carry discovery.
