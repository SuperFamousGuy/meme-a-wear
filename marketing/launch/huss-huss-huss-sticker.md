## Audience

- The Huss Town segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 12 to 25 on
  TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with Roblox, basketball, and gym crossover
  (basketball and gym: medium). Stickers are a high-confidence buy.
- A sticker is the cheapest entry to this meme, and it suits buyers under
  16 or the gift buyers who shop for them.
- Players stick it on what goes to practice: a water bottle, a gym bag, a
  laptop. Each one is a small brag about the grind.
