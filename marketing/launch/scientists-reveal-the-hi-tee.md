## Audience

- The Scientists Reveal the Hi segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  13 to 30 on Instagram Reels and TikTok, plus cat-meme fans on Reddit. The
  core 16 to 25 group buys t-shirts (profile confidence: high).
- Fans under 16 may need a parent or gift buyer to check out (profile
  confidence: high). At $21.99 the tee is the easier gift of the two products
  for this trend.
- They share one-word recaptions of the headline. The tee works as a
  wearable "hi", so it suits posts about saying hi without talking.
