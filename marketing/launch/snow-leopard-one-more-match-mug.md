## Audience

- The Snow Leopard Paws at You segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  15 to 30, with a gamer core (profile: high) who play late.
- They share the caption plus a short game request. The mug makes that
  request at the desk during a session.
- Mug buying is `contested` for buyers under 30 in the profile. Mugs look
  stronger as gifts, so frame posts as a gift for the friend who always asks
  for one more match.

## Channels

Use organic posts only. As of 2026-10-18 the store had no TikTok, Instagram,
or Pinterest account. The user creates each one if it still doesn't exist.

1. **TikTok.** The main platform for this audience (profile: high). A clip
   of the mug at a desk at night, with a "one more match" caption, fits the
   gamer core. Create a business account.
2. **Instagram.** The meme spreads as an image post (brief). One panel holds
   the paw and the caption, so any desk photo shows the full joke. Create a
   professional account.
3. **Pinterest.** No brief puts this audience on Pinterest (profile: high).
   Some stale reports suggest Pinterest suits desk and mug posts. Pin the
   mockup for "gamer gift" searches. Low priority.

X carries the meme too, but this plan covers only the three channels above.
