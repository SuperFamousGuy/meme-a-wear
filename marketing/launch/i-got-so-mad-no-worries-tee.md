## Audience

- The I Got So Mad segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to 25 on
  TikTok, comment-section regulars (petty acts as big events: high). Tees
  are a high-confidence buy.
- The "no worries" reply fits students and young workers aged 18 to 25 who
  answer every annoyance politely. Group-project chats and work email are
  the same joke.
- Fans share it by tagging the coworker or classmate who says "no worries"
  through clenched teeth.
