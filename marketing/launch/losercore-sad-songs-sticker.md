## Audience

The buyers are the Losercore segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 15
to 28, indie and alt listeners (confidence: low). They buy laptop and
notebook stickers (high). Many are under 18, and a sticker is the one item in
their pocket-money range.

They share the music side of the joke: the loner turns the sad songs up.
A sticker on a laptop, guitar case, or phone is a prop they show in their own
videos. That gives the product a second life after the buyer receives it.

## Posts

Use the brand voice. Never say "playlist", never tag Spotify, and never name
or tag the promoter. Don't name any song, band, or album.

### TikTok

No clip exists yet. The user films or approves each clip. You can score a
clip with `virality_predictor`. The score is an estimate. Sounds must come
from the Commercial Music Library. Connect the account and run
`tiktok_music_trending` with genre `LO-FI` to list cleared options. A slow,
quiet track suits the joke. Avoid well-known sad songs, which aren't
licensed for business accounts.

- **Hook A.** Shot: a closed laptop, then a slow push-in on the sticker.

Add the size, finish, and material lines from the Printify product after you

**Price:** $4.50, with shipping charged to the buyer at the Printify rate.
Single stickers sell for about $3 to $5 on Etsy. Free shipping would lose
money (about $3.99 to ship one, podvector, checked 2026-10-18). The tee and
sticker may come from different Printify providers, so combined shipping
isn't guaranteed. Check this in Printify before you promote them as a pair.
