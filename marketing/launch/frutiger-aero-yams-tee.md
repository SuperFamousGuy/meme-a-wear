## Audience

- The Frutiger Aero Yams segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  25 on TikTok, X, and Instagram who follow brainrot slang (profile:
  medium) and the Y2K aesthetic (low). The core buyer is 16 to 25 (high), and
  they buy t-shirts (high).
- Fans share the caption with the arrow and the literal glossy yam. The tee
  carries both, so wearing it is the joke. They share it to point at a
  friend standing to their right.
- Fans under 16 may need a parent or gift buyer (high). At $19.99 the tee is
  an impulse buy for older fans and an easy gift for younger ones.
- People who don't know the meme read it as a Y2K aesthetic shirt (brief).
  That's a bonus, not the target.
