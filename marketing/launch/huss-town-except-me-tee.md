## Audience

- The Huss Town segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 12 to 25 on
  TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with Roblox, basketball, and gym crossover
  (basketball and gym: medium; gamers: high). Tees are a high-confidence
  buy.
- Buyers under 16 may not buy a $21.99 tee on Etsy themselves (high). Aim
  the listing at the 16 to 25 core and at gift buyers for younger players.
- The joke flatters the lone grinder. Players share it to brag about the
  practice nobody saw, or to tag the friend who never goes out.

## Posts

All copy follows the brand voice: dry, short, and never explaining the
meme. Never use the creator's audio, animation, or avatar. Never show or
trace a lip-dub creator. Never use the Roblox name or logo. Never name or
tag the original creator. Film the product, a ball, and an empty court only.

A ball bounces into frame alone. On-screen text, one line at a time:
