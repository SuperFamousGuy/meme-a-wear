## Audience

- The Lucia Will Appreciate This segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
  ages 16 to 35, gamers on X and Instagram, plus couples (gamers: high;
  couples: medium). Tees are a high-confidence buy.
- Dog owners are the second buyer. The profile rates the dog-owner claim
  low, from one stale brief, so treat this segment as a test.

  from the partner who always walks the dog, to the one who doesn't. Dog
