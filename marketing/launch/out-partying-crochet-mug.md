## Audience

- The While They're Out Partying segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
  ages 13 to 25 on TikTok and Instagram Reels. Fast-food workers post the
  burger-wrapping version, the trend's biggest clip.
- Mug buying is `contested` in the profile, and weaker for under-30 buyers.
  This mug is for working fans with a shift break, and for friends who buy a
  gift for the coworker who folds too neatly.
- They share mock pride in a skill nobody needs. A neat burger stack under
  the caption is that pride, on a mug.

## Posts

All copy follows the brand voice: dry, short, and never explaining the
meme. Never use a frame or audio from the viral clips, and never name or tag
their creators. Never show a fast-food chain's wrapper, logo, uniform, or
restaurant. Use plain paper and a plain table.

**TikTok concept 1, "the format."** Overlay the caption "While they're out
partying btw" in TikTok's default text. Film a tall stack of sandwiches
wrapped in plain paper with crisp folds. End on the mug beside the stack.
- Caption: `Perfect folds. Every single one.`
- Hashtags: `#whiletheyreoutpartying #btw #fastfood #burger #funnymug`

the person failing. Same caption over one badly wrapped sandwich that falls
open. Cut to the mug.
- Caption: `Still practicing.`

folds too neatly." Mug on a break-room table.
