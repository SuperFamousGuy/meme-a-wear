  30, on TikTok and Instagram Reels. The profile says they buy laptop and
  notebook stickers (confidence: high).

  the line.

  sticker leaves the decision open, so the buyer supplies it.

| Views per post | TikTok, Instagram | One post above 5,000 views |

- **Shipping check.** If visits are high but orders are near zero, the
  $3.99 shipping line is the likely cause. Report it, and the user can
  revisit the shipping setup or ask the designer for a multi-sticker pack.
- **First ad test signal.** Don't advertise the sticker alone. Each sale
  clears under $2. If the line as a whole meets the tee or mug ad signal,
  include the sticker in that test.
- **Pull signal.** Stop posting on 2026-11-15, or sooner if the brief turns
  `saturated` or `dead`. If the proof shows the hands filling in, deactivate
  the listing until the designer fixes the art. Otherwise leave it up as an
  add-on until its 4-month renewal (about 2027-02-21). Renew only if it sold
  in the last 30 days. Deactivate it at once if Etsy receives an IP
  complaint.
