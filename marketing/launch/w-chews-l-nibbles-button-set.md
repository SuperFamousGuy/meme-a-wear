## Audience

- The W Chews / L Nibbles segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  25 who use W and L slang, plus food posters (food segment: medium). They
  buy laptop and notebook stickers (high).
- Students decorate laptops, bottles, and lunch boxes. The pair lets them
  stick each verdict where it belongs, which gives them something to post.
- At $5.99 the set is an impulse buy, and an add-on for mug buyers.
