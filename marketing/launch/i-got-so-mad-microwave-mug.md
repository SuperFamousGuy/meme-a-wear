## Audience

- The I Got So Mad segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to 25 on
  TikTok, comment-section regulars and compilation viewers (petty acts as
  big events: high).
- Mug buying is contested for buyers under 30 in the profile. Aim it as a
  cheap desk gift for students and young office workers who reheat the same
  coffee three times.
- Fans share by adding their own line. The mug invites that: "I got so mad
  I ___" in the comments.
