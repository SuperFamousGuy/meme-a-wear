This is the fastest and cheapest way to sell the meme while it peaks. The
caption carries no gamer request, so it reaches cat-meme fans too, and it
works as an add-on to the hoodie.

The spec asks for a die-cut. A kiss-cut gives the same shaped sticker on a
backing sheet and costs less than half as much. At the die-cut cost, the
margin is under $1. Pick a kiss-cut provider if the shape allows it. A sticker
with free shipping loses money, so charge shipping.

The caps caption needs the user's brand approval. That doesn't count against
viability.
