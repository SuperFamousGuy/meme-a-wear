2026-10-26, and Halloween is Saturday. The pitch has no artwork, and the
store had no Etsy shop as of 2026-10-18. Production plus transit puts
delivery after 2026-10-31, when the brief says the format stops landing.

Out of season, the design is the stronger of the two haunted-house pitches.
Students are the core buyer, and laptop stickers are a proven buy for them.
If the meme-expert verifies a seasonal revival, the designer can re-pitch it
in early September 2027.

A sticker with free shipping loses money. Charge shipping, or sell it as an
add-on. The brand approval for the caption is pending. That doesn't count
against viability.
