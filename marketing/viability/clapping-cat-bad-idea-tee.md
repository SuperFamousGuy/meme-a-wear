The tee is the flagship of the clapping-cat line and clears the bar easily.
I viewed both variants and both mockups. The caption is spelled correctly,
only brand colors appear, and the dark variant's Yolk "LOVE IT." stands out
well. The cat's half-closed eyes differ from the brief's round eyes, but the
grin and hands carry the joke. The generic caption is its main weakness. It
works on any occasion, but it's less shareable than a specific one.

Production fixes come before the first order: an upscale from 207 DPI, the
dark outline at about 0.10 in against the 0.12 in minimum, and recording the
visual check in the spec. The old `light.svg` and `dark.svg` files are still
in the folder. Brand approval is still pending.
