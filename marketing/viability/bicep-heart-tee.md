Nothing is wrong with this pitch, but nothing is strong either. Every score
is a 2, and the total misses the bar by two.

The trend is a video pose. Its fans know it from people doing it on camera.
A flat icon of a gesture written from text alone may read as a plain flex or
generic heart hands. The brief has one dated source and no still, and a
follow-up pose is already pulling attention. A tee costs two variants and two
mockups to test that.

The sibling sticker tests the same icon for less, and carries no text. If the
sticker sells and the brief's confidence rises, the designer can re-pitch the
tee.

The draft guidelines need the user's brand approval. That doesn't count
against viability.
