The art is strong and the caption is open-ended, so buyers supply their own
bad decision. Print-on-demand shipping is the weak point. Etsy sticker
sellers often mail stickers for about a dollar, so a $3.99 shipping line
makes this look expensive next to them. Two options keep the margin. You can
list it as a cart add-on, which costs about $0.50 to ship with a tee or mug.
You can also offer a multi-sticker pack, if the designer adds more captions.
The user chooses the shipping setup.

Ask for a print proof before a run. The thin Smoke lines on the hands may
fill in at 3 in, and the hands carry half the joke. Brand approval is still
pending.
