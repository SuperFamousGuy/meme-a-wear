The sticker is the faithful, cheap version of a meme that already has a GO
tee and a GO mug. It needs no word swap and no explanation, and it works as
an add-on to either.

Timing is the risk. The brief found no new spread since mid-October, and the
poll result looks back on October. A sticker suits that: it lists fast, costs
little to test, and needs no large run. Recheck the brief before any large
order.

Safety scores 2 for the same reason as the tee: the drawn cat must not read
as a trace of the BBC-owned photo. Charge shipping. The serif-headline and
fur-color exceptions need the user's brand approval. That doesn't count
against viability.
