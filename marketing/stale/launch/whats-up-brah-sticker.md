## Audience

The sticker is for the "What's Up Brah?" segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
ages 13 to 35 on TikTok and Reels, South Park fans and gamers (confidence
`low`, one brief). The cross-trend claims that matter here:

- They buy laptop and notebook stickers (`high`).
- The core buyer is 16 to 25 (`high`). A $4.50 sticker is the cheapest way in.
- Part of the audience is under 16 (`high`). A sticker fits a teen's budget
  or a parent's add-on.

What makes them share: the sticker has no opponent. The buyer picks one by
where they stick it. "Stick him facing your X" is the meme's swap-the-opponent
remix, done by the buyer. Every post invites the next opponent.

## Posts

Rules for every post:

- Never use "Randy", "Marsh", "South Park", "Sharon", "Broadway Bro Down",
  "Comedy Central", or "bro down" in captions, hashtags, on-screen text, or
  alt text. Never name or tag the meme's creator or uploaders.
- Never use the episode audio or anyone's "What's up brah" original sound.
- **Opponents stay everyday objects**: laptops, fridges, alarm clocks,
  inboxes, water bottles, homework. Never stick him facing a person, a
  photo of a person, or anything political. This is the sticker's main open
  risk.
- Post the mockup as a product image. Don't present it as a customer photo.

**Sounds.** The TikTok account doesn't exist yet, so `tiktok_music_trending`
can't run. After the user connects it, pick a Commercial Music Library track
and confirm its CML label. Every post below works muted.

**Virality score.** No clip exists yet. `virality_predictor` can score a clip
that the user films or approves. Treat the score as an estimate.

**Price:** $4.50 USD. The buyer pays shipping. Single vinyl stickers sell for
about $3 to $5 on Etsy (RankHero, April 2026). Expected net is about $2.30.
Set the shipping price to Printify's sticker shipping cost, so the store
doesn't lose money. Free shipping on a single sticker loses money.

**Listing tasks:**

- user: List the Printify print provider as a production partner in Etsy.
- user: Check Etsy's current creativity standards for AI-assisted designs and add a disclosure if Etsy requires one.
- user: Consider opting out of Etsy Offsite Ads. A 15% fee on $4.50 is about $0.68 of a $2.30 margin.
- user: Mention the sticker in the tee and mug listings as a matching add-on, if the user wants to cross-sell. Don't claim a bundle discount the shop doesn't offer.
- designer: Remove "bro down" from `copy.tags` in the spec. The listing tags above already leave it out.
- designer: Add a true-scale mockup (sticker next to an everyday object for scale, no hands) and a clean art export without the Ink waste area.
- designer or printer: Set the cut line on the outer edge of the Chalk border and add 0.125 in bleed (see `open_risks`).

## Measures

| Measure | Target by 2026-11-04 (14 days) |
|---|---|
| Etsy listing views | 100 or more |
| Favorites rate | 5% or more |
| Orders | 2 or more, alone or with the tee or mug |

| Best "today's opponent" post | 1,000 views or more |

**Success:** 2 or more orders by 2026-11-04, or any order that pairs the
sticker with the tee or mug.

**Pull the product** when any of these happens:

- Etsy or a rights holder sends an IP complaint. Deactivate at once.
- A buyer photo or comment shows the sticker aimed at a person or a political
  target and it spreads. Pause posts and review with the user.
- The brief turns `dead` or `do-not-use`.
- By 2026-11-15, the listing has no orders and fewer than 50 views.

**First small ad test signal:** none on its own. The $2.30 margin can't carry
ad spend. If the tee meets its ad test signal, the user can consider an ad for
the tee with the sticker as an add-on. The ad budget is $0, so the user
decides.
