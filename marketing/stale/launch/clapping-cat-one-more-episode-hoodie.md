- The Black Cat Clapping segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages 13 to
  30, on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

  (confidence: medium). Late-October launch fits that season.

  Gift buyers in November and December are a second segment.

  "One more episode at 2 a.m." comes straight from the brief's list of
  captions that land.
