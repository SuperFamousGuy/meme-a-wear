## Audience

The tee is for the "What's Up Brah?" segment in `marketing/target-market.md`:
ages 13 to 35 on TikTok and Reels, South Park fans and gamers (confidence
`low`, one brief). The cross-trend claims that matter here:

- The core buyer is 16 to 25 (`high`), and they buy t-shirts (`high`).
- Part of this audience is under 16 (`high`). Parents and gift buyers may
  place those orders, so the listing names gift uses.
- The humor is ironic, absurd, and loud (`high`). Mock fight-card hype is
  this trend's tone.

What makes them share: a "mascot vs. X" face-off they can stage with the
product (viability reports, `low`). The alarm clock is the first fight of
every day, so the post is relatable to anyone who hits snooze. Fans read the
stance and caption at a glance. Don't explain the meme.

## Posts

Rules for every post:

- Never use "Randy", "Marsh", "South Park", "Sharon", "Broadway Bro Down",
  "Comedy Central", or "bro down" in captions, hashtags, on-screen text, or
  alt text. Never name or tag the meme's creator or uploaders.
- Never use the episode audio or anyone's "What's up brah" original sound.
  A TikTok business account can use only the Commercial Music Library (CML).
- Opponents stay everyday objects. No people, no political images.
- Post the mockups as product images. Don't present a mockup as a customer
  photo.

**Sounds.** The TikTok account doesn't exist yet, so `tiktok_music_trending`

works muted with on-screen text.

**Success:** the listing meets the views and orders targets by 2026-11-04.
Then keep posting until the stop date and add new opponents to the rematch
series.

**Pull the product** when any of these happens:

- Etsy or a rights holder sends an IP complaint. Deactivate at once and don't relist.
- The brief turns `dead` or `do-not-use`.
- By 2026-11-15, the listing has no orders and fewer than 50 views.

**First small ad test signal:** 3 or more organic orders, or a conversion rate
of 2% or more on at least 150 views, by 2026-11-04, while the brief is still
`peaking`. The ad budget is $0, so this is only a signal. The user decides
whether to fund a test.
