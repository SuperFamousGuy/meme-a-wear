## Audience

- The Scientists Reveal the Hi segment in `marketing/target-market.md`: ages
  13 to 30 on Instagram Reels and TikTok, plus cat-meme fans on Reddit
  (cat-meme segment: high). They buy laptop stickers (high).
- The sticker is the unchanged original meme, so it's the cheapest entry and
  an add-on for buyers of the tee or the coffee mug.
- They share one-word recaptions and "hi" moments. A sticker that says hi on
  a laptop fits posts about not wanting to talk.
