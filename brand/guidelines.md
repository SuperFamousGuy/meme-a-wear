# meme-a-wear brand guidelines

status: draft

These guidelines are a minimal first draft. They cover the core identity and
the rules the first product design needs. The user has not approved them yet.
Every spec that uses them stays `draft` until approval.

## Logo

meme-a-wear uses a lowercase wordmark set in Archivo Black. A Yolk bar sits
under the "a" in the middle of the name.

| File | Use on |
|---|---|
| `brand/logo.svg` | Light backgrounds: Chalk, white, ash, sand |
| `brand/logo-reverse.svg` | Dark backgrounds: Ink, black, navy, charcoal |

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "m".
- Set the logo at least 0.75 in wide in print and 96 px wide on screen.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
- Don't put the logo on the front print of a product. Use it on neck labels,
  hang tags, packaging, and the storefront.

## Color palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#141414` | Main dark. Text and artwork on light surfaces. Fills on all garments. |
| Chalk | `#F2F2EE` | Main light. Text and artwork on dark surfaces. |
| Yolk | `#F2C14E` | The only accent. Eyes, sparks, highlights, one emphasis word. |
| Smoke | `#6B6B66` | Storefront secondary text on Chalk. In product artwork, only for non-text shading: fur highlights and edge lines inside Ink shapes, plus thin details such as whiskers. |

Contrast rules:

- Ink on Chalk is about 16:1. Chalk on Ink is the same.
- Yolk on Ink is about 11:1, so Yolk text is fine on dark surfaces.
- Yolk on Chalk or any light garment is under 2:1. Never set Yolk text on a
  light surface. Use Yolk only for non-text shapes there.
- Smoke on Chalk is about 4.7:1. Use it only for body-size text or larger.
- Don't use pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

| Font | Use | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archivo Black | Logo, product captions, headlines | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo+Black |
| Archivo (400, 600) | Storefront body text, labels, product copy | SIL OFL 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo |

- Set product captions in Archivo Black, all caps, with tight but legible
  tracking.
- Keep a caption to eight words or fewer, so it reads from across a room.
- Printers convert text to outlines before printing.

## Voice

meme-a-wear sounds like the friend who encourages your worst idea and means
it. The voice is dry, short, and in on the joke. It never explains the meme.

- **Short.** Captions use two to six words. Descriptions stay under 50 words.
- **Deadpan.** State the absurd thing as plain fact.
- **Kind at the core.** Laugh at a situation or at yourself. Never at a group,
  a person, or a tragedy.
- **Honest.** Never promise prices, shipping times, materials, or stock unless
  the store confirms them.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| BAD IDEA. LOVE IT. | OMG this cat is SO relatable lol!!! |
| Applauds your choices. All of them. | The viral TikTok cat everyone is talking about! |
| A cat with a grin that is a little too human. | Premium quality, ships in 2 days! |
| Wear it to the meeting you should have skipped. | Name or tag the creator or uploader of the original clip. |

## Product rules

- Draw all artwork as original vector art. Don't trace, copy, or reference
  frames from source videos or images.
- Never depict an identifiable real person.
- Garment fronts use Ink, Chalk, and Yolk, plus Smoke for non-text shading.
  Never set caption text in Smoke. Each garment design has a light variant and
  a dark variant.
- On dark garments, give Ink shapes a Chalk outline at least 0.12 in thick,
  so they don't disappear into the fabric.
- Follow the print rules in the `design-spec` skill for sizes, line weights,
  and contrast.

## Storefront rules

- Use Chalk as the page background and Ink for text. Use Yolk for the primary
  action only.
- Use Archivo Black for headlines and Archivo for everything else.
- Storefront screens are out of scope for this draft. Expand this section when
  storefront work starts.
