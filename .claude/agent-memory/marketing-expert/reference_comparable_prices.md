Collected 2026-10-18. All figures come from third-party sources. Confirm them in the Printify editor and on Etsy's fee page.

- Etsy POD averages (Listadum, July 2026): tee $20.17, mug $17.81. Single vinyl sticker about $3-5 (RankHero, April 2026).
- Printify base: Bella+Canvas 3001 about $10.50-11 Free / $8.77 Premium; 11 oz mug $4.69-8.50 by provider, plus $4.39-7.99 shipping; 3x3 kiss-cut $1.20-1.52.
- Re-check 2026-10-18 (later run): tee base $10.95-11.69 Free + ~$5 ship; mug $5.50-8.25 + $4.55 ship; kiss-cut from EUR 1.24 (Printify catalog). Fees unchanged.
- Pet bandana: Etsy "puppy bandana" median about $15, 63% at $10-25 (RankHero, May 2026). Printify Evergreen tie-on from EUR 12.40 / EUR 8.99 Premium; clip-on from EUR 7.60. Bandana shipping not found.
- Mug landed cost: the first figures give $9.08-16.49, and the later re-check gives $10.05-12.80. Provider choice decides mug margin.
- TikTok business accounts are likely limited to the Commercial Music Library and shouldn't use other users' original sounds (Fox Music 2026 guide). Hurts audio-led memes.
- Hoodie: Printify Gildan 18500 about $21.58 Free (podvector, May 2026), US shipping $5.35-7.49 (plan $6.50). POD hoodie range $35-65 (insightagent.app, weak vendor guide). $36.99 nets about $4.95.
- Sticker shipping about $3.99 first, $0.50 each extra (podvector). A $4.50 sticker with free shipping loses money. Charge shipping or sell it as an add-on.
- Tee US shipping about $4.75. At $21.99 a tee nets about $3.70. Mug at $16.99 is profitable only with a low-cost provider.
- Etsy US fees: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction (incl. shipping), 3% + $0.25 processing. Offsite Ads 15% when attributed.

Access: etsy.com search, etsy.com/legal/fees, redbubble.com, and teepublic.com return 403 to WebFetch. Amazon search returns 503. The Printify product pages load without prices (JS). Use WebSearch summaries and state the gap.
