Marketing's 2026-11-09 reviews set three defaults for new pitches:

1. Stickers are kiss-cut contour, not die-cut. A die-cut base (~$3.78) loses money at $4.99; kiss-cut (~$1.21) keeps ~$2.40.
2. Keep registered marks (such as "Frutiger") out of `copy.title` and `copy.tags`. Etsy IP reports key on them. A printed caption may still use the meme phrase.
3. On a mug, put the meme's image and its caption on the same panel (repeat the panel on both sides). One view or desk photo must show the whole joke.

**Why:** Each cost a REVISE round or a non-blocking action on snow-leopard-one-more-match-mug, frutiger-aero-yams-*, and bicep-heart-sticker.

**How to apply:** Apply these in the first draft of every pitch. Related: [[meme-text-vs-brand]].
