Since 2026-10-26, pitches flag two recurring brand conflicts for the user to decide. (1) Caption style: brand captions are Archivo Black, all caps, eight words or fewer. But "Scientists reveal the Hi" needs a serif headline, "While they're out partying btw" needs sentence-case outlined sans, and the haunted-house template runs past eight words. Added 2026-11-02: "W chews / L nibbles" relies on capital-letter-then-lowercase casing (pitches keep it), and "*paws at you*" is lowercase with asterisks (pitches use caps, keep asterisks). Added 2026-11-24: "will appreciate this" pitches keep a sentence-case tag line and run 12 to 14 words; "i got so mad" pitches use caps instead of the meme's sentence case; the huss sticker/tee caption plus "huss huss huss" runs nine words. (2) Palette: Added 2026-11-24: the GTA pop-up's navy and pink become Ink and Yolk, and the huss figure's royal-blue outfit became Ink. But marketing judged the Ink figure generic, so on 2026-11-24 the huss tee restored a royal blue (Huss Blue #2F5BD3, outfit only, no text) as a pending palette exception. Lesson: when an off-palette color carries recognition, keep it as a flagged exception rather than recoloring. Ink, Chalk, Yolk, and Smoke have no ginger or pink. The pitches render ginger fur in Yolk.

**Why:** Brand guidelines are still draft. Only the user can approve exceptions.

**How to apply:** Check `brand/guidelines.md` for a decision before making artwork from those pitches. When the user rules, record it here as feedback and drop the "pending" wording.
