On 2026-10-18, the whats-up-brah alarm clock tee needed three attempts. Attempt 1: a bald man in a yellow wrap robe with a black belt read as a Buddhist monk or martial-arts gi. Attempt 2: a black sleep mask read as side-swept hair, and with a small square mustache the face evoked a notorious dictator. Attempt 3 passed: a yellow mask with drawn closed eyes, a hooded terry bathrobe with a matching sash, and a wide walrus mustache.

**Why:** These readings fall under the extremist_or_coded and cultural risks, even when the brief found none. The model doesn't avoid them unless the prompt does.

**How to apply:** In any prompt with a mustache, ask for a wide or bushy shape and add "NOT a small square or toothbrush mustache". Avoid dark shapes on the forehead that could read as hair. Give costumes explicit domestic cues (hood, pocket, fabric sash) so a robe can't read as religious or martial. See [[reference-prompting]].
