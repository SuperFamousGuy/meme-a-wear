Marketing's REVISE on the W chews pitch (2026-11-02) scored a two-button set 1 on margin, and the pitch became a sticker set, which then rated GO.

**Why:** Buttons cost too much per unit for their price point.

**How to apply:** For a small add-on item, pitch a sticker or die-cut magnet before a button. Pitch a button only with a reason that offsets the margin.
